name: "Vision"
cat: "M"
summary: "How well a player sees passes and possibilities that others do not."
---

Vision determines whether a player spots the through ball, the switch of play or the runner. It is key for the Ball-Playing Goalkeeper, Advanced Centre-Back, Pressing Wing-Back, Deep-Lying Playmaker, Box-To-Box Playmaker, Midfield Playmaker, Advanced Playmaker, Playmaking Winger and Free Role. Creators with low Vision tend to play safe passes, while those with high Vision can unlock defences.
