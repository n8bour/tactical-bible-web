---
key: "acc"
name: "Acceleration"
cat: "F"
summary: "How quickly a player reaches top speed from a standing start or a change of direction."
---

Acceleration is about the first five yards, not the sprint over thirty. It decides whether a winger gets away from a full-back, whether a defender recovers after being turned, and whether a pressing player closes the ball carrier before the pass leaves. Roles that live on short bursts, such as the Winger, Inside Winger, Wing-Back, Sweeper Keeper and Pressing Defensive Midfielder, list it as a key attribute. Covering and wide centre-backs benefit too, because they have to react to a runner who is already moving.
