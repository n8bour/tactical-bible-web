name: "Crossing"
cat: "T"
summary: "How accurate a player is when delivering the ball from wide areas."
---

Crossing decides whether the ball arrives where your box runners expect it. It is a key attribute for Wing-Backs, Advanced Wing-Backs, Wide Midfielders and Wingers, and a preferred one for Full-Backs, Wide Central Midfielders and Wide Forwards. A crossing-heavy plan wastes its numbers if the crosser has weak Crossing, so match team crossing instructions to the player.
