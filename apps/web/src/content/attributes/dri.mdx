name: "Dribbling"
cat: "T"
summary: "How well a player runs with the ball and beats opponents in one-on-one situations."
---

Dribbling is the key skill for ball carriers: Wingers, Inside Forwards, Wide Forwards, Free Roles, Channel Midfielders, Wing-Backs and even False Nines. It lets them take on a defender instead of laying the ball off. Pair it with Agility and Balance, and be careful with a team-wide dribbling instruction if only a few players can actually do it.
