summary: "How quickly a goalkeeper reacts to shots."
---

Reflexes is the keeper's shot-stopping reaction. It is key for the Goalkeeper, No-Nonsense Goalkeeper, Line-Holding Keeper and Sweeper Keeper, and preferred for the Ball-Playing Goalkeeper. It becomes more valuable when your defence concedes close-range or deflected shots.
