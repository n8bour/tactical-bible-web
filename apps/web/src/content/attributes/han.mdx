name: "Handling"
cat: "G"
summary: "How securely a goalkeeper catches and holds shots."
---

Handling decides whether a save is held or spilled for a rebound. It is a key attribute for the Goalkeeper, No-Nonsense Goalkeeper and Line-Holding Keeper, who stay close to their line and face shots more often. Together with Reflexes it forms the core of shot-stopping.
