name: "Communication"
cat: "G"
summary: "How well a goalkeeper talks to and organises the defence in front of them."
---

Communication is a goalkeeper-only attribute that helps the back line stay organised and aware. It is a preferred attribute for the Goalkeeper and No-Nonsense Goalkeeper, and a nice extra for a Sweeper Keeper working with a high line. It rarely makes a keeper good on its own, but a vocal keeper behind a young or mixed back line is a real asset.
