name: "Composure"
cat: "M"
summary: "How calm a player stays on the ball and in front of goal when under pressure."
---

Composure is what keeps passes accurate and finishes clean when an opponent is closing in. It is a key attribute for the Ball-Playing Goalkeeper, Sweeper Keeper, Ball-Playing Centre-Back, Inside Full-Back, Deep-Lying Playmaker and Half Back, all of whom receive the ball in tight areas. It also helps forwards and Advanced Playmakers who get little time to think. Build-up through a press depends heavily on it.
