name: "Teamwork"
cat: "M"
summary: "How well a player works within the team's shape and plan."
---

Teamwork is the cooperation attribute: it keeps players in their tactical positions and supports their roles in the system. It is key for Full-Backs, Wing-Backs, Defensive Midfielders, Deep-Lying Playmakers, Half Backs, Box-To-Box Midfielders and Central Midfielders. A player with low Teamwork drifts away from the shape, so avoid asking them to fill a disciplined supporting role.
