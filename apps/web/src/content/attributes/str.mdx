---

Strength supports hold-up play and defensive duels. It is key for Centre-Backs, Ball-Playing Centre-Backs, No-Nonsense Centre-Backs, Stopping Centre-Backs, Target Forwards, Deep-Lying Forwards and Centre Forwards. It also helps Dropping Defensive Midfielders and Half Backs who shield the back line.
