Tackling is the key defensive skill for Centre-Backs, Full-Backs, Wing-Backs, Defensive Midfielders and Stopping Centre-Backs. It reduces both missed challenges and fouls, so it matters most for aggressive players and for teams that press. High Aggression without Tackling is a dangerous combination.
