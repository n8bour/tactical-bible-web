key: "aer"
name: "Aerial Reach"
cat: "G"
summary: "A goalkeeper's reach when coming for crosses, high balls and corners."
---

Aerial Reach is the goalkeeper's equivalent of Jumping Reach: how much of the box the keeper can claim in the air. It matters most for the Goalkeeper and No-Nonsense Goalkeeper, who are expected to dominate crosses rather than rely on defenders, and it helps the Sweeper Keeper when the line is high and long balls drop behind it. A keeper with low Aerial Reach is better paired with tall, strong centre-backs and a team that limits crosses.
