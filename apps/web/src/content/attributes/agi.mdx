---
key: "agi"
name: "Agility"
cat: "F"
summary: "How nimbly a player changes direction and turns in tight spaces."
---

Agility lets a player twist away from a marker, switch direction while dribbling, or react quickly in goal. It is a key attribute for the Winger, Inside Winger, Inside Forward and the Goalkeeper and Line-Holding Keeper, and a useful extra for the Wing-Back, Advanced Playmaker and Free Role. Dribblers with high Agility but low Balance look quick yet get knocked off the ball, so read the two together.
