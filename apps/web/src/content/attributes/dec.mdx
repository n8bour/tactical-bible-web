key: "dec"
name: "Decisions"
cat: "M"
summary: "How good a player is at choosing the best option from the ones available."
---

Decisions is the attribute behind passing, dribbling or shooting at the right moment, and it shapes defensive choices such as when to step out or hold. It is a key attribute for the Ball-Playing Goalkeeper, Sweeper Keeper, Ball-Playing Centre-Back, Advanced Centre-Back, Inside Wing-Back, Pressing Wing-Back, Defensive Midfielder and Deep-Lying Playmaker. Players who start attacks from deep need it more than most, because every wrong choice there is dangerous.
