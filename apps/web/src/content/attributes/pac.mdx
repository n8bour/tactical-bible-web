---
key: "pac"
name: "Pace"
cat: "F"
summary: "How fast a player is at top speed."
---

Pace helps in recovery and in running behind. It is key for Overlapping Centre-Backs, Covering Centre-Backs, Covering Wide Centre-Backs, Advanced Wing-Backs, Wingers, Wide Forwards, Channel Forwards and the outlet roles, and a preferred attribute for Centre-Backs and Wing-Backs. A fast defender allows a higher line, while a slow one makes a high line dangerous.
