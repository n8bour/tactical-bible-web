summary: "How high a player can reach when jumping for the ball."
---

Jumping Reach is the physical part of winning aerial balls and is key for Centre-Backs, No-Nonsense Centre-Backs and Target Forwards. It works with Heading for accuracy and Bravery for willingness. Tall, high-reaching defenders are valuable when you defend deep against crosses.
