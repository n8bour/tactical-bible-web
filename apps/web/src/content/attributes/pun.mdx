name: "Punching (Tendency)"
cat: "G"
summary: "A goalkeeper's tendency to punch the ball away rather than catch it."
---

Punching (Tendency) describes how likely a keeper is to punch crosses and high balls instead of catching them. It is a style indicator rather than a quality attribute: punching is safer in crowded boxes, catching keeps possession. No role lists it as key, so choose it to fit how your keeper behaves under crosses.
