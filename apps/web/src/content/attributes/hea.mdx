summary: "How well a player wins and directs headers."
---

Heading matters for the player who contests high balls and attacks crosses. Centre-Backs, Ball-Playing Centre-Backs, No-Nonsense Centre-Backs, Inside Full-Backs, Poachers and Target Forwards list it as key. It combines with Jumping Reach and Strength: all three together make a reliable aerial target, while one alone is usually not enough.
