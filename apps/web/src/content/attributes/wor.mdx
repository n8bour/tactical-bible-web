---
key: "wor"
name: "Work Rate"
cat: "M"
summary: "How much effort a player puts into working, closing down and covering ground."
---

Work Rate decides how hard a player chases and tracks back during a match. It is key for Overlapping Centre-Backs, Wing-Backs, Box-To-Box Midfielders, Pressing Wing-Backs and Pressing roles, and preferred for Full-Backs and Defensive Midfielders. A pressing system needs it across the team; a playmaker who gets away with low Work Rate should be protected by the players around them.
