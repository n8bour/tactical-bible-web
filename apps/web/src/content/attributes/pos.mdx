---

Positioning is the defensive reading of the game: being in the right place before the ball arrives. It is key for goalkeepers and centre-backs, Full-Backs and Inside Full-Backs. It also supports Wing-Backs, Defensive Midfielders and Deep-Lying Playmakers. A good Positioning player needs less pace and effort to defend well.
