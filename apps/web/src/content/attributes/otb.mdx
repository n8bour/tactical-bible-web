---
key: "otb"
name: "Off the Ball"
cat: "M"
summary: "How well a player finds space and times runs when their team has the ball."
---

Off the Ball is the attribute that makes forward runs worthwhile. It is key for Advanced Wing-Backs, Box-To-Box Midfielders, Wide Central Midfielders, Channel Midfielders, Advanced Playmakers, Attacking Midfielders, Free Roles and Second Strikers. Runners with poor Off the Ball arrive too early, too late or in an offside position, so pair it with Anticipation and pace.
