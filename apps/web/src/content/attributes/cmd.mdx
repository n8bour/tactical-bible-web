name: "Command of Area"
cat: "G"
summary: "How well a goalkeeper controls and organises the penalty area."
---

Command of Area covers claiming crosses and corners and taking charge of the box, rather than waiting on the line. It is a key attribute for the Sweeper Keeper and a preferred one for the Goalkeeper and No-Nonsense Goalkeeper. It matters most when your defence is high or you concede a lot of crosses, because a commanding keeper cleans up what the defenders cannot.
