name: "Technique"
cat: "T"
summary: "How skilful a player is at handling the ball and executing technical actions."
---

Technique is the general ball-craft attribute behind controlled passing, dribbling and shooting. It is key for Advanced Centre-Backs, Inside Wing-Backs, Deep-Lying Playmakers, Midfield Playmakers, Wide Central Midfielders, Advanced Playmakers and Wingers. It matters most for creators and for any role asked to play in tight spaces.
