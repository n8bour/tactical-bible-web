name: "Determination"
cat: "M"
summary: "How much extra effort and resilience a player shows, especially when things go badly."
---

Determination keeps a player working through poor spells, fatigue and setbacks. No role in the guide lists it as key, so it works as a general-quality attribute: useful in tight matches, for rotation players and for squad leaders. Look at it when choosing a long-term core, as it tends to separate reliable players from talented ones who drift out of games.
