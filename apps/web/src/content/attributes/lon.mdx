key: "lon"
name: "Long Shots"
cat: "T"
summary: "How well a player strikes shots from outside the box."
---

Long Shots determines whether a shot from range is dangerous or wasted. It is a key attribute for the Attacking Midfielder and preferred for the Box-To-Box Midfielder, Box-To-Box Playmaker, Channel Midfielder, Free Role, Second Striker and Inside Forward. Use it alongside the team long-shots instruction: encourage shots from distance only if the players taking them have the attribute.
