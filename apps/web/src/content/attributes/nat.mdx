key: "nat"
name: "Natural Fitness"
cat: "F"
summary: "How well a player's body copes with training load, fatigue and recovery."
---

Natural Fitness is a physical attribute: it influences recovery and how tired a player gets over a match and a season. It is not a key attribute of any role, but it supports high-intensity ones such as Wing-Backs, Box-To-Box Midfielders and Pressing roles. Use it as a tiebreaker when picking players for a heavy schedule.
