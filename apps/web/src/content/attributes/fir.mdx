name: "First Touch"
cat: "T"
summary: "How cleanly a player controls the ball when it arrives."
---

First Touch decides whether a pass is controlled in one movement or bounces away. It is key for Ball-Playing Goalkeepers, Advanced Centre-Backs, Inside Wing-Backs, Deep-Lying Playmakers, Box-To-Box Playmakers, Midfield Playmakers, Advanced Playmakers and Attacking Midfielders. Roles that receive the ball under pressure between the lines need it most, and it supports a fast, short passing style.
