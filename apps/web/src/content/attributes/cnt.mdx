name: "Concentration"
cat: "M"
summary: "How long a player can stay focused and keep their positioning without switching off."
---

Concentration is the defender's attention span: it stops mistakes late in matches and keeps marking tight when the play is far from them. Goalkeepers, Centre-Backs, Covering Centre-Backs, Full-Backs and Defensive Midfielders all rely on it, as do Ball-Playing Centre-Backs and Inside Full-Backs. If your back line makes odd late errors, check this first.
