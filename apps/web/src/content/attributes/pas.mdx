name: "Passing"
cat: "T"
summary: "How accurate and weighted a player's passes are."
---

Passing is the core attribute for ball distributors: Ball-Playing Goalkeepers, Ball-Playing Centre-Backs, Advanced Centre-Backs, Inside Wing-Backs, Deep-Lying Playmakers, Box-To-Box Midfielders, Central Midfielders and Midfield Playmakers. It matters most in a possession style with short passing, where every pass counts. Pair it with Vision for creativity and Composure for passing under pressure.
