key: "fre"
name: "Free Kick Taking"
cat: "T"
summary: "How well a player strikes direct free kicks."
---

Free Kick Taking determines the quality of direct and delivered free kicks. It is a set-piece attribute rather than a role attribute, so no role lists it as key. Choose your free-kick taker by this together with Technique and Long Shots, and consider putting the best taker on the pitch even if he is not the best open-play player.
