summary: "How far and how accurately a goalkeeper kicks the ball."
---

Kicking matters when the keeper distributes with long goal kicks or punts. It is a key attribute for the Ball-Playing Goalkeeper and No-Nonsense Goalkeeper, and a preferred one for the Goalkeeper and Sweeper Keeper. Match it to your goal-kick instruction: a keeper with poor Kicking should not be asked to launch the ball to a target forward.
