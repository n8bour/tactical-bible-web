summary: "How well a goalkeeper deals with an attacker running through on goal."
---

One on Ones is a goalkeeper attribute that matters when your line is high or you concede breakaways. It is key for the Goalkeeper and Sweeper Keeper, and preferred for the Line-Holding Keeper. A high defensive line without a strong one-on-one keeper is risky.
