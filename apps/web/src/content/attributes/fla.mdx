---
key: "fla"
name: "Flair"
cat: "M"
summary: "How inclined a player is to try the unexpected or spectacular."
---

Flair raises the willingness to attempt creative or risky actions rather than the safe pass. It is key for the Attacking Midfielder, Playmaking Winger and Free Role, and useful on Midfield Playmakers, Advanced Playmakers and attacking wide players. Too much Flair in a disciplined role such as a holding midfielder works against the role, so keep it for your creators.
