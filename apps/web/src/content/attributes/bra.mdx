---
key: "bra"
name: "Bravery"
cat: "M"
summary: "How willing a player is to put their body on the line in challenges, headers and blocks."
---

Bravery shows up in aerial duels, blocks and last-ditch tackles, so it matters for No-Nonsense Centre-Backs, Stopping Centre-Backs and Target Forwards. It also supports ball-winning midfielders such as the Pressing Defensive Midfielder and Pressing Central Midfielder. A player short on Bravery tends to avoid the contact their role demands, so do not ask them to play a physical, front-foot role.
