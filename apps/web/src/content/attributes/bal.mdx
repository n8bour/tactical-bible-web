---
key: "bal"
name: "Balance"
cat: "F"
summary: "How well a player stays upright and keeps control when challenged or off-balance."
---

Balance decides whether a player rides a shoulder charge or goes to ground. Target Forwards list it as a key attribute because they take contact with their back to goal, and it supports dribbling roles such as the Winger, Free Role, Inside Forward and False Nine, as well as playmakers like the Deep-Lying Playmaker who shield the ball. Combine it with Strength for hold-up play and with Agility for dribblers.
