name: "Rushing Out (Tendency)"
cat: "G"
summary: "How readily a goalkeeper comes out of their area to deal with danger."
---

Rushing Out (Tendency) describes how likely a keeper is to leave the area to cut out through balls. It is key for the Sweeper Keeper, who has to cover the space behind a high line. A keeper with high values suits a high defensive line, while a keeper who stays home suits a deeper block.
