---
key: "agg"
name: "Aggression"
cat: "M"
summary: "How readily a player goes into challenges and chases the ball, for better or worse."
---

Aggression raises the willingness to close down and tackle, which is why Stopping Centre-Backs, Stopping Wide Centre-Backs, Pressing Full-Backs and Pressing Defensive Midfielders rely on it. The cost is discipline: an aggressive player with poor Tackling or Decisions will give away fouls and get pulled out of position. Use it where you want front-foot defending, and keep it modest in covering and holding roles.
