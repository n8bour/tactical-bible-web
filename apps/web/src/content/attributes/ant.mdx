---
key: "ant"
name: "Anticipation"
cat: "M"
summary: "How well a player reads the game and reacts before a situation fully develops."
---

Anticipation is the defender's early warning: it helps the Sweeper Keeper, Covering Centre-Backs, Full-Backs and Defensive Midfielders step into passing lanes and arrive before the attacker. It also helps attackers time runs, which is why it pairs with Off the Ball. Players with high Anticipation can compensate for modest pace, so it is a good attribute to hunt for in older or slower defenders.
