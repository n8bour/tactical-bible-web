---
key: "ecc"
name: "Eccentricity"
cat: "G"
summary: "A goalkeeper's tendency to do unpredictable or risky things."
---

Eccentricity describes how willing a goalkeeper is to leave the line or try something unorthodox. It is not a simple good or bad attribute: it can produce spectacular sweeping and also embarrassing errors. Treat it as a risk indicator when choosing a Sweeper Keeper or Ball-Playing Goalkeeper, and be cautious with high values on a keeper who also has poor Decisions.
