---
key: "fin"
name: "Finishing"
cat: "T"
summary: "How well a player converts chances in and around the penalty area."
---

Finishing is the main attribute for goalscorers: Poachers, Second Strikers, Centre Forwards, Channel Forwards, Deep-Lying Forwards, Target Forwards, Inside Forwards and Wide Forwards all list it as key. It matters most for central strikers who take many shots from inside the box. For a creator, quality from range is covered separately by Long Shots.
