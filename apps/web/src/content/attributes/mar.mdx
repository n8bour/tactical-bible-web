---
key: "mar"
name: "Marking"
cat: "T"
summary: "How well a player stays close to and blocks the opponent they are marking."
---

Marking governs tight man-marking and tracking of runners, and is key for Centre-Backs, No-Nonsense Centre-Backs, Overlapping Centre-Backs, Full-Backs, Inside Full-Backs, Wing-Backs, Holding Full-Backs and Defensive Midfielders. It matters most in a man-marking or tight-marking setup, and less for covering players who prefer to hold a zone.
