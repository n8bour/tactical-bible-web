key: "sta"
name: "Stamina"
cat: "F"
summary: "How long a player can keep running at intensity during a match."
---

Stamina determines how long a player keeps working before they fade. It is key for high-effort roles such as Overlapping Centre-Backs, Wing-Backs, Box-To-Box Midfielders, Wide Central Midfielders and Pressing roles. A pressing, high-tempo plan needs high Stamina across the team or substitutions around the sixtieth minute.
