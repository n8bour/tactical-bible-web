summary: "How well a player delivers corner kicks."
---

Corners sets the quality of the ball into the box from a corner, and is mostly relevant to set-piece takers rather than to any role's open-play behaviour. No role in the guide lists it as a key attribute, so choose your corner taker from the squad by this attribute together with Crossing and Technique. Pair a good taker with strong aerial targets for the best results.
