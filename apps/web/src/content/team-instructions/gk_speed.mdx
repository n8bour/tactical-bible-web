## What it changes

Goalkeeper distribution speed.

GK Distribution Speed sets how quickly the keeper releases the ball after catching or collecting it. Distribute Quickly looks for an immediate outlet, which suits a counter-attack before the opposition recovers; Slow Pace Down waits for the team to move into position. In the lab it changes how soon the restart happens after the keeper has the ball.

## Who needs which attributes

There are no required attributes here. The keeper's Kicking, Throwing and Decisions help the quick option find a good outlet, and the outfield players need to be moving into space straight away. If the team is stretched or out of shape after a long attack, slowing down gives it time to reorganise.

## Pros / cons

- **Pros:** Quick feeds counter-attacks.
- **Cons:** Quick with the team out of position loses the ball.
