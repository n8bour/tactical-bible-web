## What it changes

Press the opposing keeper’s short distribution (High Press section).

Short Goalkeeper Distribution tells the team whether to press when the opponent's keeper plays short. Yes pushes forwards and midfielders up onto the keeper and the nearest centre-backs so the opponent has to go long; No leaves them in shape. In the lab it sends the forwards towards the keeper while he has the ball. It is new in FM27, and its effects are still being verified.

## Who needs which attributes

No attributes are listed. The press needs forwards with Work Rate, Acceleration and Stamina, and the defensive line must move up with them. If it does not, the opponent can play a long ball into the space behind. Use it against teams that insist on playing short, not against those with a strong target forward.

## Pros / cons

- **Pros:** Yes forces the opponent to go long.
- **Cons:** It means pushing strikers and midfielders up — more space in behind.
