## What it changes

Pass into space or to feet (new in FM26).

Pass Reception decides whether passers aim at the receiver's feet or into the space ahead of them. Pass to Feet produces shorter, controlled passes that the receiver has to take under pressure; Pass into Space leads the receiver and favours through balls, which creates depth and speed but needs the runner to arrive on time. In the lab it changes how far ahead of the runner the pass is aimed and how attractive through balls look.

## Who needs which attributes

To feet needs players who can receive safely: First Touch, Composure and Technique, especially against a high press. Into space needs runners who move first and move fast: Off the Ball and Acceleration, with Pace to finish the run. A team with technical midfielders and a static striker should choose feet; a team with quick forwards against a high line should choose space.

## Pros / cons

- **Pros:** To feet: control. Into space: depth and speed.
- **Cons:** To feet against a high press demands First Touch/Composure. Into space demands Off the Ball/Acceleration.
