## What it changes

Where the ball carrier is forced (mid block/high press).

Pressing Trap decides which side of the pitch the press forces the ball carrier toward. Outside tucks wide players in so the ball goes to the flank, where the touchline helps the press; Inside keeps the wide players wider, so the ball is pushed into the crowded centre. In the lab it moves the wide players' lines accordingly.

## Who needs which attributes

The data lists no attributes; the roles matter. Outside needs full-backs and wingers who press aggressively and quickly so the trap closes on the flank; Inside needs a strong, compact midfield with good Tackling, Positioning and Work Rate to win the ball once it arrives. Without those players the trap is open and the opponent goes through it.

## Pros / cons

- **Pros:** Outside: corners them on the flank. Inside: forces them into a crowded middle.
- **Cons:** Outside without aggressive full-backs/wingers does not close the trap; Inside needs a strong midfield.
