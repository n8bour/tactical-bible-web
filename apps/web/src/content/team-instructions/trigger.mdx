## What it changes

How often players trigger the press.

Trigger Press controls how readily players leave their position to close the ball down. More triggers means a bigger press radius around the ball and a more aggressive team; Less means holding the shape and waiting for the opponent to come to you. In the lab the press radius scales by about 20 % per step.

## Who needs which attributes

Pressing is physically demanding, so Work Rate and Stamina come first. Aggression makes players commit, Anticipation helps them time the trigger, and Teamwork keeps the press coordinated so one player does not run alone. Uncoordinated pressing is easily beaten, so if the squad is tired or lacks Teamwork, reduce it.

## Pros / cons

- **Pros:** More: high recoveries, chaos for the opponent.
- **Cons:** Physically draining; uncoordinated pressing is easily beaten.
