## What it changes

Type of cross.

Crossing Style sets how the ball is delivered from wide areas: Low Crosses are driven along the ground, Whipped Crosses are fast and flat, and Floated Crosses are slow and high. In the lab the setting changes the ball flight of crosses, so you can see whether the delivery arrives at head height, along the ground or hangs.

## Who needs which attributes

The match-up is between delivery and targets. Low crosses suit quick, sharp strikers who finish with Finishing and Off the Ball. Floated crosses need aerial forwards and late runners with Heading, Jumping Reach and Strength. The crosser's Crossing and Technique decide how accurate the cross is whatever the style.

## Pros / cons

- **Pros:** Low for quick strikers; Floated for aerial targets.
- **Cons:** Floated without players strong in the air is a waste.
