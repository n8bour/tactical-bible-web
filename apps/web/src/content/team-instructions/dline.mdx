## What it changes

Height of the defensive line.

Defensive Line sets how high the back line stands. A higher line compresses the pitch and moves the opponent further from goal; a lower line gives up the middle of the pitch but protects the space behind. In the lab the line moves from about 22 m to 46 m from the own goal, and the team's other lines follow it.

## Who needs which attributes

A high line needs defenders who can handle being turned: Pace and Acceleration to recover, Anticipation to read the through ball, and Positioning to stay in line. A sweeper keeper helps cover the space behind. A low line needs less athleticism but more heading and tackling in the box, because the opposition will have the ball closer to your goal.

## Pros / cons

- **Pros:** High: compact team, opponent far from goal. Low: protects the space in behind.
- **Cons:** High needs quick centre-backs and a Sweeper Keeper. Low invites pressure and shots.
