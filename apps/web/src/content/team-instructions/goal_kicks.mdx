## What it changes

Type of goal kick.

Goal Kicks sets what the keeper does when the ball is out of play. Short goal kicks start the build-up from the back and invite a press that you hope to play through; long goal kicks skip the opposition's first line and aim for a target or the space behind. In the lab it decides between short, mixed and long keeper distribution.

## Who needs which attributes

No attributes are listed because the decision is mainly about the people who take and receive the kick. Short kicks need a keeper and centre-backs with Passing, Composure and First Touch; long kicks need a target who wins the aerial duel (Heading, Jumping Reach, Strength) and teammates who contest the second ball. Match it to your build-up and to whether the opponent presses high.

## Pros / cons

- **Pros:** Short: starts the build-up. Long: skips the press to the target.
- **Cons:** Short against a high press with defenders weak on the ball is risky.
