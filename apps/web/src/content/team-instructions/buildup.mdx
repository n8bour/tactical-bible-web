## What it changes

Play out short through the press or bypass it (new in FM26).

Build-up Strategy is the plan for getting out of your own third against a press. Play Through Press keeps passes short, uses the keeper and centre-backs, and tries to draw opponents forward before breaking them; Bypass Press goes longer early and reduces the number of risky passes at the back. In the lab it adjusts pass length, keeper distribution and how high the team sits during build-up.

## Who needs which attributes

Playing through the press needs technical defenders and a keeper who can pass: Passing, Composure, First Touch and Decisions are what keep a pass under pressure from becoming a goal against. Bypassing needs the opposite quality up front: a forward who wins the first or second ball and teammates close enough to take it. If your back line is weak on the ball, do not play through the press.

## Pros / cons

- **Pros:** Through: draws in and breaks the press. Bypass: minimum risk at the back.
- **Cons:** Through with technically weak GK/centre-backs = goals conceded. Bypass loses possession.
