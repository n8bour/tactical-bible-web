## What it changes

How often players carry/dribble the ball.

Dribbling sets how often players carry the ball and take on defenders instead of passing. Encourage raises the attractiveness of the carry option for everyone; Discourage lowers it so the ball moves quickly by passing. In the lab each step moves the weight of the carry option by a small amount, so you can watch players take on a defender more or less often.

## Who needs which attributes

Encouraging dribbling needs players who win one-on-ones: Dribbling, Agility and Flair, with Balance and Acceleration helping them stay on their feet. Without those, you will see turnovers in dangerous areas. Discouraging it suits sides built on short passing and movement, where ball carriers are not expected to beat their man.

## Pros / cons

- **Pros:** Encourage with quality dribblers.
- **Cons:** Encouraging with poor Dribbling = turnovers in dangerous areas.
