## What it changes

Preferred zone for progressing the ball.

Progress Through gives the team a preferred side for moving the ball forward: Left, Right or through the Centre. Players favour passing and carrying in that zone, and the shape leans slightly toward it. In the lab choosing Centre narrows the shape a bit, while Left or Right biases the flank used for progression.

## Who needs which attributes

No attribute is needed; it is about where your best players and the opponent's weaknesses are. Point it toward your most creative winger or full-back pairing, or at the side where the opponent's full-back is weakest. Because it is predictable, make sure the other side still offers a threat or the opponent will overload the favoured one.

## Pros / cons

- **Pros:** Focus on the side of your best player or the opponent’s weak spot.
- **Cons:** Predictable; the opponent can overload that side.
