## What it changes

Allow or stop crosses (low block).

Cross Engagement is a low-block setting that decides whether defenders challenge crosses or accept them. Stop Crosses makes full-backs and wing-backs close wide carriers harder before the delivery; Invite Crosses lets them drop off and defend the box. In the lab Stop Crosses raises the pressure on wide carriers.

## Who needs which attributes

No attribute is listed, but the choice depends on your defenders. Invite Crosses needs centre-backs who win the ball in the air: Heading, Jumping Reach, Strength and Bravery. Stop Crosses suits full-backs with Pace, Tackling and Stamina who can reach the carrier in time, usually when the centre-backs are short.

## Pros / cons

- **Pros:** Invite: with centre-backs dominant in the air. Stop: with short defenders.
- **Cons:** Invite without centre-backs strong in the air = headed goals.
