## What it changes

Freedom to deviate from the plan.

Creative Freedom decides how closely players stick to the plan versus trying something different. More Expressive settings lower the team's risk aversion and nudge players toward through balls and ambitious dribbles; More Disciplined settings make them take the safer, expected option. In the lab each step shifts risk appetite by a modest amount, so the effect is clearest on the decision panel, where riskier options climb the ranking.

## Who needs which attributes

Expressiveness only pays off when players can choose well and see the opportunity, so Decisions, Flair and Vision are the attributes that matter. A team of high-Flair, high-Decisions creators gets a real return from freedom; a squad of workmanlike players with low Decisions mostly turns the freedom into giveaways. If only one or two players have the quality, give them the freedom through their role and player instructions rather than a team-wide setting.

## Pros / cons

- **Pros:** Expressive: top players find solutions. Disciplined: predictable, safe execution.
- **Cons:** Expressive with players weak in Decisions/Flair = chaos.
