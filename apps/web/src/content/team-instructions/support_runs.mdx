## What it changes

Full-backs/wing-backs support inside (underlap) or outside (overlap).

Supporting Runs decides how full-backs and wing-backs join the attack: Overlap sends them outside the winger, Underlap sends them inside the winger into the half-space. In the lab it adds overlapping or underlapping runs in the final third for those roles, which in turn changes where the winger takes the ball.

## Who needs which attributes

There is no attribute requirement in the data, but the pairing matters more than any number. Overlap works best with a winger who cuts inside (an inside forward or inverted winger), because the full-back takes the width he leaves. Underlap works best with a winger who stays wide, so the lane is occupied and the full-back arrives in the gap. The runner needs Stamina, Off the Ball and Acceleration to keep doing it.

## Pros / cons

- **Pros:** Overlap with inside wingers; underlap with wide wingers.
- **Cons:** It clashes with the role: a W with overlaps crowds the channel.
