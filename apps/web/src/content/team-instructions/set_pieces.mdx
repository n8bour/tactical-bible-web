## What it changes

Try to win, or avoid winning, corners and free kicks (new in FM27).

Play for Set Pieces tells wide and attacking players to favour actions that win corners and free kicks, such as crossing and driving at defenders, while Keep Ball in Play avoids them. In the lab it is modelled as a small shift toward crossing in the final third. The option names and effects are new in FM27 and not fully confirmed, so treat the lab behaviour as an estimate.

## Who needs which attributes

There are no required attributes in the data, but the setting only makes sense if the payoff exists. That means strong aerial players (Heading, Jumping Reach, Strength, Bravery) to attack the delivery, and a good taker with Corners, Free Kicks, Crossing and Technique. If your side has neither, keeping the ball in play is almost always better.

## Pros / cons

- **Pros:** Play for Set Pieces pays off with good headers and set-piece takers.
- **Cons:** Keep Ball in Play keeps the tempo but wastes a weapon if you are strong in the air.
