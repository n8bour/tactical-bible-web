## What it changes

Aggression in the tackle.

Tackling sets how committed defenders are in the tackle. Get Stuck In wins the ball more often but raises the chance of fouls and bookings; Stay On Feet keeps defenders on their feet, jockeying instead of diving in. In the lab each step moves the team's tackle commitment, which changes both the number of won duels and the fouls.

## Who needs which attributes

The aggressive setting suits players with high Tackling, Aggression and Bravery, because they win the ball more than they give away fouls. Defenders with low Tackling who commit will foul and get booked. If the team has weak tacklers, Stay On Feet is safer.

## Pros / cons

- **Pros:** Get Stuck In wins more duels.
- **Cons:** More fouls and cards; dangerous with defenders who have low Tackling.
