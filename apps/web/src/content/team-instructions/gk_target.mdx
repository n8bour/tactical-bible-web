## What it changes

Where the goalkeeper distributes.

GK Distribution tells the keeper whom to look for first. Centre-Backs and Full-Backs keep the ball short, Flanks sends it to the wide players, and Target Forward goes long to the striker. In the lab the setting maps onto short, mixed or long keeper distribution; Balanced leaves the choice to the keeper.

## Who needs which attributes

No single attribute applies; pick the outlet that is strongest in your team. A technical centre-back needs Passing, First Touch and Composure; a quick wing-back wants Pace and Acceleration; a target forward needs Heading, Jumping Reach, Strength and Bravery. Because the destination becomes predictable, make sure the player you pick can cope with being pressed on arrival.

## Pros / cons

- **Pros:** Direct the ball to your strength (a technical centre-back, a quick wing-back, a tall striker).
- **Cons:** Predictable: the opponent can set a trap.
