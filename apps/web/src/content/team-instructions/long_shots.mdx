## What it changes

How often players shoot from outside the box.

Shots from Distance sets how often players try to shoot from outside the area. Encourage makes them take speculative efforts instead of passing; Discourage keeps the ball moving until a closer chance appears. In the lab the shot option gains or loses some utility per step, and it is considered from further out when encouraged.

## Who needs which attributes

This works best with players who can strike the ball cleanly: Long Shots and Technique are the key attributes, with Composure helping under pressure. It is most useful against a low block that leaves space in front of the box, and with good rebound hunters in support. A team of poor long-range shooters gains nothing from it.

## Pros / cons

- **Pros:** Encourage with good shooters against low blocks.
- **Cons:** Poor shots waste possession.
