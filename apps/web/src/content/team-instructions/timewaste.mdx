## What it changes

Managing the clock.

Time Wasting changes how much the team slows the game: More makes players take longer over restarts and be slower to release the ball, while Less keeps the game flowing. In the lab it lengthens decision time slightly and slows restarts, so you see the team consume the clock without changing its shape.

## Who needs which attributes

There are no required attributes. It is a game-management setting for protecting a lead, and it works best with a settled, patient side that keeps possession comfortably. Use it late in a match when you are ahead; leaving it on early only slows your own attack and can attract bookings and angry opponents.

## Pros / cons

- **Pros:** Useful when winning late on.
- **Cons:** Can irritate opponents/the referee; breaks your own team’s rhythm.
