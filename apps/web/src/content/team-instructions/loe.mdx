## What it changes

Where the team starts pressing.

Line of Engagement sets how far up the pitch the team starts pressing: Low Block waits near the own box, Mid Block starts around halfway, and High Press starts in the opposition half. In the lab it moves the point where the block engages by roughly 16 m per step. It was reworked in FM27, so the option names and effects may still shift a little as the game is analysed.

## Who needs which attributes

High settings are demanding: Work Rate, Stamina, Acceleration and Aggression are what let players close down quickly and keep doing it. If the defensive line does not move up with the press, big gaps appear between the lines, so the line height and the engagement need to match. A low block needs more discipline than energy: Positioning, Teamwork and Concentration for the defensive shape.

## Pros / cons

- **Pros:** High: wins the ball near the opponent’s goal. Low: compact block, invites them out.
- **Cons:** High demands Stamina/Work Rate; disconnected from the defensive line it opens space between the lines.
