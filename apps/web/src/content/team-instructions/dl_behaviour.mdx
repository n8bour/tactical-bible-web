## What it changes

Whether the line drops to protect depth or steps up to squeeze space (new in FM27).

Defensive Line Behaviour decides whether the back line steps up to squeeze space or drops to protect depth when the ball moves. Step Up pushes the line higher and catches players offside; Drop Off goes deeper to cover the space behind. In the lab it shifts the line by about 2 m up or down. The option names and effects are new in FM27 and not fully confirmed, so treat the lab behaviour as an estimate.

## Who needs which attributes

No attributes are listed, but the line itself needs to suit it. Step Up needs quick centre-backs with Pace, Acceleration, Anticipation and Positioning, ideally with a sweeper keeper. Drop Off suits slower defenders, but the gap to midfield grows unless the midfield drops as well.

## Pros / cons

- **Pros:** Step Up: compact team, catches players offside. Drop Off: protects the space in behind.
- **Cons:** Step Up with slow centre-backs is dangerous; Drop Off separates the defence from midfield.
