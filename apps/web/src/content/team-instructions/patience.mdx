## What it changes

Deliver early crosses into the box or patiently work the ball into shooting positions.

Final Third Patience sets how quickly the team delivers the ball into the box. Hit Early Crosses sends it in before the defence is set; Work Ball Into Box recirculates until a shot or a short pass into the area opens up. In the lab each step moves the weight of crossing versus patient play, and more patience also reduces long shots.

## Who needs which attributes

No required attributes, but the targets matter. Early crosses need wide players with Crossing and forwards who attack the delivery with Heading, Jumping Reach and Off the Ball. Patient play needs midfielders and forwards who can combine in tight spaces, with Passing, Technique, Composure and Decisions. Against a deep block the patient option can stall without creative players.

## Pros / cons

- **Pros:** Work Ball Into Box: higher-quality chances. Hit Early Crosses: catches the defence before it sets.
- **Cons:** Work Ball Into Box against a low block can produce sterile possession; Hit Early Crosses wastes possession without good targets.
