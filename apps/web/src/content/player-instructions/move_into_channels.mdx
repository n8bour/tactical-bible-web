## What it changes

Looks for the space between centre-back and full-back.

The player looks for the space between the centre-back and the full-back and makes his runs there. In the lab it adds channel runs during progression and in the final third.

## Who needs which attributes

Quick strikers against high lines.

Pace, Acceleration, Off the Ball and Anticipation are the attributes that matter, so that the run starts at the right moment and does not end in an offside position. It works best for quick strikers against high defensive lines.

## Pros / cons

- **Pro:** Gets the player behind the defence and creates one-on-ones for the keeper.
- **Pro:** Stretches the back line and makes the defenders choose whom to follow.
- **Con:** If the pass does not come, he is out of the build-up and the team loses a link.
- **Con:** More offsides if his timing is poor or the line is well organised.
