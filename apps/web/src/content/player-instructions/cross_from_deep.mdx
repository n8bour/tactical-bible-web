## What it changes

Crosses earlier, from deeper.

The wide player delivers earlier from deeper positions, before reaching the byline. In the lab cross utility rises from deep and falls near the byline, so he crosses as soon as there is a target.

## Who needs which attributes

Wing-backs and inside wingers with good Crossing.

Crossing and Technique are the key attributes. It suits wing-backs and inside wingers who may not have the pace to reach the byline but can put an accurate cross into the box.

## Pros / cons

- **Pro:** Catches the defence before it is set and suits a quick, early delivery to a striker.
- **Pro:** Avoids the risk of being tackled or trapped at the byline.
- **Con:** Crosses from deeper are easier for defenders and keepers to deal with.
- **Con:** Needs forwards who arrive in the box at the right time, or the ball goes nowhere.
