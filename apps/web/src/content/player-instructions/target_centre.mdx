## What it changes

Attacks the centre of the box on crosses.

On crosses the player attacks the centre of the box, the most contested area. In the lab his box runs go to the centre of the box. This instruction is new in FM27, so its exact in-game behaviour is not fully confirmed.

## Who needs which attributes

Aerial target.

Heading, Jumping Reach, Strength and Bravery are the attributes that matter, with Finishing and Off the Ball to get into the position. An aerial target forward is the natural fit.

## Pros / cons

- **Pro:** Puts the best aerial player where most crosses land.
- **Pro:** Occupies the centre-backs and creates space for teammates.
- **Con:** Marked by the strongest defenders, so he faces the most competition.
- **Con:** Without a good aerial player, the ball is just cleared.
