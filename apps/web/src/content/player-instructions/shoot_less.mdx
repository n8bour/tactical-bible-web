## What it changes

Looks for the pass instead of the shot.

The player looks for the pass or a better position instead of the shot, so the shooting option loses weight. In the lab he holds the ball a little longer and passes to teammates who are better placed rather than shooting at the first opportunity.

## Who needs which attributes

Players who waste possession by shooting.

Use it on players who finish poorly but still get into shooting positions: low Finishing, Long Shots or Composure combined with good Passing and Vision. It is the right setting for a creator or a hard-working forward whose strength is not goals.

## Pros / cons

- **Pro:** Stops low-quality shots from wasting attacks and hands the chance to a better finisher.
- **Pro:** Helps a creative player stay in the build-up instead of ending moves early.
- **Con:** The player may pass up a decent chance and the opponent gets time to recover.
- **Con:** If everyone follows it, the team has no one willing to shoot and attacks stall.
