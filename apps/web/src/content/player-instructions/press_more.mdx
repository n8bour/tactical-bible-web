## What it changes

Presses more than the rest of the team.

The player presses more than the rest of the team, and chases the ball from further away. In the lab his press radius is about 35 % larger than the team setting.

## Who needs which attributes

High Work Rate and Stamina.

Work Rate and Stamina are the key attributes, with Aggression, Acceleration and Anticipation helping him close the ball down. He should be a forward or midfielder who can recover afterwards.

## Pros / cons

- **Pro:** Forces mistakes and wins the ball in advanced positions.
- **Pro:** Sets the tone for the rest of the team's press.
- **Con:** Tires faster and leaves his position when the press is beaten.
- **Con:** If teammates do not follow, he is isolated and the opponent plays around him.
