## What it changes

Releases the ball earlier.

The player releases the ball earlier and passes instead of taking on the opponent. In the lab the carry option loses weight, so he plays the next pass while a defender is still approaching.

## Who needs which attributes

Poor dribbling in dangerous areas.

Use it on players with weak Dribbling, Agility or Balance who would otherwise lose the ball in dangerous areas. A deep-lying midfielder, a full-back or a target forward that passes well is the usual candidate.

## Pros / cons

- **Pro:** Reduces turnovers in risky places and keeps the team's rhythm quick.
- **Pro:** Lets a technically weaker player contribute through passing and movement instead.
- **Con:** The team loses a player who might beat a defender and create a chance.
- **Con:** Defenders can step up expecting the pass, making the play predictable.
