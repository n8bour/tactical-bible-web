## What it changes

Arrives in the box as a second wave, attacking the space that opens up.

The player arrives in the box as a second wave, after the first runners have taken the defenders. In the lab his box run starts about 1.2 s after the cross trigger, so he attacks the space that opens up. This instruction is new in FM27, so its exact in-game behaviour is not fully confirmed.

## Who needs which attributes

Box-to-Box, Attacking Midfielder, Channel Midfielder.

Off the Ball, Anticipation and Stamina are the attributes that matter, with Finishing and Composure for the end of the run. Box-to-Box and Attacking Midfielder roles fit best.

## Pros / cons

- **Pro:** Adds an unmarked runner to the box and surprises the defenders.
- **Pro:** Works well with a target forward who pulls defenders away.
- **Con:** If the delivery is early, he is not there to meet it.
- **Con:** He is a long way forward and leaves a space behind if the ball is lost.
