## What it changes

Goes to the byline before crossing.

The wide player runs to the byline before crossing, instead of delivering from further out. In the lab cross utility rises close to the byline and falls from deep, so he carries the ball further down the flank before he whips it in.

## Who needs which attributes

Quick wingers.

Pace and Acceleration get the player to the byline, and Dribbling and Crossing let him get there and still deliver an accurate cutback. Quick wingers and wing-backs are the natural fit.

## Pros / cons

- **Pro:** Produces cutbacks and low crosses into the penalty area, which are hard for defenders to deal with.
- **Pro:** Pulls defenders toward the goal line and creates space on the edge of the box.
- **Con:** Takes longer to deliver, which lets defenders recover and block.
- **Con:** A slow player gets tackled or runs out of play before the cross.
