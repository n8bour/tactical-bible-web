## What it changes

Tighter marking.

The player stays closer to his opponent and gives him less time and space. In the lab his marking distance is reduced by about 1 m.

## Who needs which attributes

Marking ≥ 15.

Marking of 15 or more is the headline attribute, with Anticipation, Positioning and Pace helping him stay with a quick opponent. Strength and Tackling help when he must win the duel.

## Pros / cons

- **Pro:** Takes away the opponent's time and makes it harder to receive and turn.
- **Pro:** Neutralises a specific danger man, such as a creator or target forward.
- **Con:** He can be pulled out of position by a clever runner.
- **Con:** Gets beaten more easily by a sharp turn, leaving a big gap behind him.
