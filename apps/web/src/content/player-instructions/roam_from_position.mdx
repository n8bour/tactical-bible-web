## What it changes

Freedom to leave his position.

The player is free to leave his assigned position and follow the ball or find space. In the lab he drifts towards the ball side and a little higher up the pitch, and his position is less predictable.

## Who needs which attributes

Only for players with high Decisions/Off the Ball; adds complexity.

Only players with high Decisions and Off the Ball should be given it, with Vision, Teamwork and Flair also helping. Without these the player wanders out of position and the team loses its shape.

## Pros / cons

- **Pro:** Gives a creative player the freedom to find space, and confuses markers.
- **Pro:** Allows a playmaker to be involved in more of the play.
- **Con:** Leaves a hole in the team's shape that the opponent can attack.
- **Con:** Makes the team harder to read for you as well, so check the balance around him.
