## What it changes

Saves energy when pressing.

The player holds his position and saves energy when pressing instead of following the ball far. In the lab his press radius is about 30 % smaller than the team setting.

## Who needs which attributes

Low Stamina.

Use it for players with low Stamina or Work Rate, or for a key attacker whose energy you want to keep for the attack. Positioning and Teamwork matter so that he still holds his shape.

## Pros / cons

- **Pro:** Saves energy for attacking and lets a tiring player last longer.
- **Pro:** Keeps the shape and avoids a hole when a player is slow to recover.
- **Con:** Leaves a gap in the press and the opponent gets time on the ball.
- **Con:** His teammates have to do more of the work, which can unbalance the team.
