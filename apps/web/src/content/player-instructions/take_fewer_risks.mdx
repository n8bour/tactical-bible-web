## What it changes

Safe passes.

The player chooses the safe pass and avoids ambitious balls. In the lab through-ball utility falls and risk aversion rises, so he keeps the ball moving sideways and backwards unless a simple option opens.

## Who needs which attributes

Centre-backs/midfielders with poor Passing.

This suits centre-backs and midfielders with poor Passing, Vision or Decisions who should not be asked to play difficult passes. A player with weak Composure under pressure also benefits.

## Pros / cons

- **Pro:** Cuts down on turnovers near your own goal.
- **Pro:** Keeps the shape and tempo stable around a limited passer.
- **Con:** The team may lose attacking penetration through that player.
- **Con:** He can become a passing outlet for the opponent to press, if everyone around him is also cautious.
