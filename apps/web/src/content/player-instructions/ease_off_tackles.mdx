## What it changes

More careful tackles.

The player tackles more carefully and stays on his feet. In the lab his tackle commitment drops, so he fouls less and wins fewer 50-50 duels.

## Who needs which attributes

Weak tackling or a booked player.

Use it for players with weak Tackling, or for a player who is already booked. Positioning, Anticipation and Pace help him defend by cover and interception instead.

## Pros / cons

- **Pro:** Reduces fouls, cards and the risk of a red card.
- **Pro:** Keeps the player on the pitch and protects the shape.
- **Con:** Wins fewer tackles, so the opponent can keep the ball more easily.
- **Con:** The opponent can take him on and he has to recover.
