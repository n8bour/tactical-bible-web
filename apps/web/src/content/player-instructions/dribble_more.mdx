## What it changes

Carries the ball and looks for the one-on-one.

The player carries the ball and tries to beat his marker in one-on-ones instead of passing early. In the lab the carry option gains weight, so he drives at defenders more and moves the ball less.

## Who needs which attributes

High Dribbling + Agility.

Dribbling and Agility are the key attributes, with Balance, Acceleration and Flair helping. A winger or attacking midfielder who wins duels turns this into a real weapon.

## Pros / cons

- **Pro:** Takes players out of the game and creates overloads and fouls in dangerous areas.
- **Pro:** Gives a team with a clear dribbler a reliable way of breaking a low block.
- **Con:** Turnovers in your own half or midfield can start a counter.
- **Con:** Slows the attack and can leave teammates standing if he loses the duel.
