## What it changes

Stays wider.

The player holds a wider lane on the pitch and stays closer to the touchline. In the lab his lane moves about 6 m wider, which you can see in the average width and shape overlay.

## Who needs which attributes

Balances asymmetric flanks.

No special attributes are needed, but he must be able to carry the width: Pace, Stamina and Crossing are useful for a winger or wing-back. It is most useful when the other flank is narrower, or when the opposition full-back needs to be pulled out of position.

## Pros / cons

- **Pro:** Stretches the opposition's block and opens space in the half-spaces.
- **Pro:** Balances an asymmetric shape where the other flank is narrow.
- **Con:** The player is farther from central play, so he is involved less in combinations.
- **Con:** Can leave a gap between him and the nearest teammate for the opponent to exploit.
