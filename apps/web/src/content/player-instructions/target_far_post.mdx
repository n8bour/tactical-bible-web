## What it changes

Attacks the far post on crosses.

On crosses the player attacks the far post, arriving at the back of the box. In the lab his box runs go to the far post. This instruction is new in FM27, so its exact in-game behaviour is not fully confirmed.

## Who needs which attributes

Far-side winger, Wide Forward.

Off the Ball, Anticipation and Finishing are the attributes that matter, with Pace and Jumping Reach helping against a high cross. A far-side winger or a wide forward is the natural fit.

## Pros / cons

- **Pro:** Finds space behind the defenders and often arrives unmarked.
- **Pro:** Works well against floated or whipped crosses from the opposite flank.
- **Con:** Needs a long cross, which is slower and easier to defend.
- **Con:** He is far from the centre, so he gets few touches unless the cross finds him.
