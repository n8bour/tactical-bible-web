## What it changes

Attacks the near post on crosses.

On crosses the player attacks the near post, running in front of the defenders to meet a low or driven ball. In the lab his box runs go to the near post. This instruction is new in FM27, so its exact in-game behaviour is not fully confirmed.

## Who needs which attributes

Quick striker, Poacher.

Pace, Off the Ball, Finishing and Anticipation are what make a near-post run work. A quick striker or poacher is the natural choice.

## Pros / cons

- **Pro:** Gets to the ball before the defenders and can finish with a flick or a touch.
- **Pro:** Pulls a centre-back away from the centre of the box.
- **Con:** Needs a low, accurate cross or the run is wasted.
- **Con:** Leaves the far post and the centre to teammates, so you need other runners.
