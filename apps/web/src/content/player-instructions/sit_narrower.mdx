## What it changes

Tucks inside.

The player tucks in, takes a more central lane and stays closer to his teammates. In the lab his lane moves about 6 m narrower, towards the centre of the pitch.

## Who needs which attributes

Helps central overloads.

No special attributes are required. A wide player who is comfortable in the middle, with Passing, Technique and Off the Ball, benefits most, especially when a full-back or wing-back provides the width on that side.

## Pros / cons

- **Pro:** Strengthens central overloads and gives teammates more passing options in the middle.
- **Pro:** Helps the team to compress the pitch and press from a compact shape.
- **Con:** Gives up the flank, so the opposition full-back gets more space.
- **Con:** Congests the middle if a winger and a midfielder both occupy the same lane.
