## What it changes

Shoots more often, including from outside the box.

The player takes the shot in more situations, including from outside the box, where he would otherwise look for a pass. In the lab the shooting option gains weight and is considered from further out, so you see more efforts and fewer final passes.

## Who needs which attributes

Long Shots ≥ 15 and high Technique.

Long Shots and Technique are the attributes that make this worthwhile, with Finishing and Composure deciding how often a shot is on target under pressure. A striker or attacking midfielder who is a real shooter benefits; a player who simply likes shooting does not.

## Pros / cons

- **Pro:** Turns a good shooter's quality into more chances, and forces the opponent to defend the edge of the box.
- **Pro:** Keeps a player in the habit of shooting when the keeper is beaten by distance or deflections.
- **Con:** Wastes possession when the shooter is poor or the angle is blocked.
- **Con:** Players who could have passed to a better-placed teammate will often shoot instead.
