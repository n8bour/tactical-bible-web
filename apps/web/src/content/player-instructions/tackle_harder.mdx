## What it changes

Harder tackles.

The player goes into tackles with more force and commitment. In the lab his tackle commitment rises, so he wins the ball more often and also fouls more.

## Who needs which attributes

High Tackling and Aggression.

High Tackling and Aggression make it work, with Bravery and Strength helping. A player with weak Tackling who tackles harder just gives away free kicks and bookings.

## Pros / cons

- **Pro:** Wins more duels and intimidates the opponent.
- **Pro:** Stops attacks early, especially in midfield.
- **Con:** More fouls and cards, and the risk of a sending-off.
- **Con:** Leaves him on the ground if he mistimes the tackle.
