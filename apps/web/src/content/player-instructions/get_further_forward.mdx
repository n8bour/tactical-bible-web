## What it changes

Gets into the final third more often.

The player gets into the final third more often and takes a higher position in possession. In the lab his target depth moves about 8 m up the pitch in possession.

## Who needs which attributes

Midfielders/full-backs with high Stamina and Off the Ball.

Stamina and Off the Ball are the important attributes, because he has to make runs and still get back. It suits midfielders and full-backs who are comfortable arriving in the box.

## Pros / cons

- **Pro:** Adds a runner to the final third, outnumbering the opposition near the box.
- **Pro:** Gives you a player who arrives late, adding unpredictable threats.
- **Con:** Leaves a space behind him if the ball is lost.
- **Con:** Tires him faster, which can show in the later part of the match.
