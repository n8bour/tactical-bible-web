## What it changes

Tries defence-splitting passes.

The player tries more defence-splitting passes and is less satisfied with the safe option. In the lab through-ball utility rises and risk aversion falls, so the decision panel often shows him preferring a through ball over a sideways pass.

## Who needs which attributes

Passing + Vision ≥ 15.

Passing and Vision of 15 or more are the attributes that matter, with Decisions and Technique helping. Vision decides whether the player even sees the through ball; Passing decides whether it arrives where it was meant to.

## Pros / cons

- **Pro:** Produces chances from a creator who sees runs others miss.
- **Pro:** Breaks lines quickly and puts forwards through on goal.
- **Con:** Loses the ball more often, especially under pressure.
- **Con:** A player without enough Vision just plays hopeful balls that give possession away.
