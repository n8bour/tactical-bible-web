## What it changes

Stays in his zone instead of following the attack.

The player stays in his zone and does not follow the attack forward. In the lab he makes no forward runs, so he stays as part of the rest-defence behind the ball.

## Who needs which attributes

Counts towards rest-defence.

It counts towards rest-defence: a full-back, wing-back or holding midfielder who stays back helps the team deal with counters. Positioning, Marking and Pace help him cover when the ball is lost.

## Pros / cons

- **Pro:** Protects the team against counter-attacks and keeps the shape tidy.
- **Pro:** Gives you a reliable rest-defence player without changing his role.
- **Con:** Removes his attacking contribution, so the team has fewer players in the box.
- **Con:** The opponent can ignore him and overload the other side.
