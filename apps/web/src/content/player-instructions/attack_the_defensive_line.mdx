## What it changes

Attacks depth on the last line.

The player holds a position on the last defender's shoulder and attacks the space behind him. In the lab he stays about half a metre behind the line and breaks forward when a pass is available. This instruction is new in FM27, so its exact in-game behaviour is not fully confirmed.

## Who needs which attributes

Quick Poachers, Channel Forwards, Inside Forwards.

Pace, Acceleration, Off the Ball and Anticipation are the attributes that matter, with Composure for finishing the one-on-one. Poachers, Channel Forwards and Inside Forwards are the natural users.

## Pros / cons

- **Pro:** Pins the defensive line back and creates chances against a high line.
- **Pro:** Gives the passer an obvious target to look for.
- **Con:** Many offsides if the timing or the team's through balls are poor.
- **Con:** Does little against a deep block, where there is no space behind the line.
