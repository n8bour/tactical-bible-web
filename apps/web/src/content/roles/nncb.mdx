## What it does by phase

Careful: clears without hesitation, takes no risks in the build-up.

He clears first and asks questions later: under any pressure he kicks the ball long or out of play instead of playing short. In build-up he stays in the back line with no ambition, which makes the team less able to keep the ball but more secure when pressed. He is the simple answer to an opponent who presses very high.

## How attributes change it

Heading, Marking, Tackling, Positioning, Strength, Jumping reach and Bravery are the real job description. Aggression is preferred because he is expected to win duels and not shy away from contact. Concentration and Anticipation help him read the danger. Passing and Technique are almost irrelevant, which is why he can be a cheap, effective choice.

## Pairs well with

Contradicts "Play Through Press". Use in a low block / direct play.

Goalkeeper options like No-Nonsense Goalkeeper, a Target Forward who wins the first ball and a Defensive Midfielder who picks up the second ball. Direct play and a low block are a natural fit. He is the safe partner next to Ball-Playing Centre-Back if you want a single distributor.

## Common mistakes

- Using him with "Play Through Press": it contradicts the plan and produces clearances that give the ball back.
- Using two of them with a technical midfield, which gets bypassed completely.
- Forgetting Aggression and Bravery; without them he is simply a worse standard Centre-Back.
