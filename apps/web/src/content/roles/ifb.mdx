## What it does by phase

Key moment (build): tucks in as a third centre-back.

In the build-up he tucks inside and becomes a third centre-back, creating a back 3 in possession. This gives the team more security in the build-up and allows the other full-back or the wingers to hold width. He stays compact and central when the team attacks.

## How attributes change it

Marking, Tackling, Positioning, Heading, Strength and Composure make him a full-back who defends like a centre-back. Passing, Decisions, Concentration and Anticipation help him as a passing option in the first line. He should be strong enough for central defending duels. In the lab, low Composure shows when he is pressed.

## Pairs well with

Great for rest-defence. The other side can have an attacking full-back.

An attacking full-back on the other side, such as an Advanced Wing-Back or Wing-Back, with the Inside Full-Back for security. A winger who stays wide provides the width he has given up. A Defensive Midfielder who stays high allows the shape to be a 3-2.

## Common mistakes

- Using him on both sides, where nobody provides width.
- Picking a full-back with weak heading for a role that includes central duties.
- Playing him with a winger who drifts inside, so no one is wide.
