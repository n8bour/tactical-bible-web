## What it does by phase

Key moment (oop): steps out to press from defensive midfield.

Out of possession he steps up from the defensive-midfield position to press the ball. He leaves a gap in front of the back line when he does, so the rest of the team has to be set to cover it. In a high-pressing or Gegenpress style he is one of the first to close down the opposition's midfield.

## How attributes change it

Work Rate scales how far he will go to close the ball and how far he tracks, so it is the most important attribute for this role. Aggression and Tackling set how hard he commits to the challenge, with the risk of fouls if his Tackling is low. Anticipation lets him step out at the right moment, and Acceleration and Stamina let him make the sprint and repeat it. Teamwork and Decisions help him know when pressing is the right call.

## Pairs well with

Gegenpress. Leave another midfielder covering.

- A Screening Central Midfielder or Defensive Midfielder beside him to hold the gap he leaves behind.
- A high defensive line and a high line of engagement, so the press is supported by the whole team.
- Forwards who press and cut the passing lanes, so he is closing down a player with limited options.

## Common mistakes

- Pairing him with another pressing midfielder, which leaves nobody in the middle when the press is bypassed.
- Using him with low Stamina. The press needs to be repeated for 90 minutes and he will run out of energy.
- Combining him with a deep defensive line, which stretches the gap behind him to a dangerous distance.
