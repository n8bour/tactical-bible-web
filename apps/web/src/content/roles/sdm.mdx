## What it does by phase

Key moment (oop): closes central passing lanes.

He holds a narrow position just in front of the back line and closes the central passing lanes into the forwards and the number ten. He refuses to be dragged out of position: if the ball moves wide he stays central. That discipline protects the space the opposition most wants to use, generally called zone 14, and makes the block hard to play through.

## How attributes change it

Positioning, Concentration, Decisions and Anticipation are key. Positioning decides how accurately he stays in the right screening spot, and Concentration keeps his perception of opponents sharp. Decisions and Anticipation determine how well he reads which lane the passer will use. Marking, Tackling and Teamwork are preferred because they help him when an attacker does enter his zone.

## Pairs well with

Protects zone 14.

- A Pressing Central Midfielder or Box-To-Box Midfielder next to him: the partner presses, he screens the middle.
- A mid or low block with compact lines, so the lanes he closes are not the only protection.
- Full-Backs who stay wide, covering the flanks while the screener holds the middle.

## Common mistakes

- Using him against a team that plays through the wings. He will protect the middle while the real danger is on the flank.
- Expecting him to win the ball back high. He is cautious by design and presses very little.
- Selecting a player with weak Positioning and Concentration. The role depends entirely on staying in the right place.
