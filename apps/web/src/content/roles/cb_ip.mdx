## What it does by phase

Holds position in the build-up, simple passes to a midfielder or full-back.

He holds the central position in the back line during build-up and plays simple passes to the nearest midfielder, full-back or goalkeeper. He does not step into midfield or carry the ball forward, so the rest-defence stays complete. Once the team is in the final third he stays around the halfway line to keep the shape.

## How attributes change it

Heading, Marking, Tackling, Positioning, Strength and Jumping reach make him a defender first, and those are what the role is built on. On the ball, Composure and Decisions decide how well he handles a simple pass under pressure, while Passing is only preferred, not critical. Anticipation and Pace matter because he is the player who must recover when possession is lost in an advanced area.

## Pairs well with

The "default" that never compromises the rest-defence.

A Ball-Playing Centre-Back on the other side: one passer, one defender is the most stable pair. A Defensive Midfielder or Half Back who drops to receive gives him the simple pass he needs. Full-Back or Wing-Back on the flank adds a wide option.

## Common mistakes

- Putting two of them together with a team that wants to play out through the press; nobody takes the progressive pass.
- Expecting him to create; Passing and Vision are not his job.
- Forgetting he still needs Composure; a simple pass under pressure can end in a goal.
