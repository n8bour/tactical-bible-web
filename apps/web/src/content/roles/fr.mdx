## What it does by phase

Key moment (prog): drifts to the ball side.

The Free Role does not hold a fixed slot. In build-up he stays high enough to be an outlet between the lines, once the ball is progressing he slides towards wherever it is, and in the final third he keeps floating into pockets of space instead of committing to the box. That constant movement creates overloads on the ball side, but it also means his own position is empty when possession is lost, so the team has to be built to cover for him.

## How attributes change it

The role lives on the ball in tight spaces, so First Touch and Technique decide whether he can control a pass received with a defender on his back and still play forward. Vision sets how far and how wide he scans for passes and whether he even sees the through ball, while Decisions sets how quickly he chooses and how often he picks the best option rather than a safe one. Flair pushes him towards the ambitious option, which is exactly what you want from a creator but costs possession if he is also poor at Passing or Composure. Off the Ball matters for the moments he drifts away from the ball to find a pocket rather than coming to it.

## Pairs well with

He works best when someone else supplies the structure he abandons. Pair him with a Deep-Lying Playmaker or Defensive Midfielder who holds the base, a Box-To-Box Midfielder who covers the space he leaves, and wide players such as a Winger (IP) or Advanced Wing-Back who keep the pitch wide while he drifts inside. A Poacher or Centre Forward (IP) up front keeps the centre-backs pinned so he has room between the lines.

## Common mistakes

- Giving the role to an average technical player. Without elite First Touch, Technique and Vision the roaming just turns into aimless wandering and lost possession.
- Using him with a second roaming creator such as a False Nine. Nobody is left to occupy the centre-backs or attack the box, and the shape becomes unbalanced.
- Surrounding him with no holding midfielder. When he drifts and the ball is lost, the opposition counter-attacks straight through the space he vacated.
- Setting a very direct, high-tempo style. The Free Role needs time to receive and turn, and those instructions bypass him.
