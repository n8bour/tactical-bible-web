## What it does by phase

Contributes defensively in the opponent’s half.

This is the default defensive duty for an attacking midfielder. He joins the team press when the ball is in the opposition half, cutting passing lanes to the holding midfielders and closing down when his zone is triggered, but he does not drop deep to defend like a Tracking Attacking Midfielder. When the opposition gets past the first wave he stays relatively high, ready to receive the ball when it is won back.

## How attributes change it

Work Rate scales how far he goes to close down and how far he follows a runner, so a low-Work-Rate player will press only when the ball is right next to him. Teamwork controls how closely he keeps the tactical position instead of chasing the ball, which keeps the press connected to the rest of the team. Anticipation sets how soon he reacts to a change in the ball, and Decisions determines whether he picks the right moment to step in rather than breaking the shape too early.

## Pairs well with

It is the base option, so it fits most systems. It works best alongside other forward-line pressers such as a Tracking Centre Forward and a Pressing Central Midfielder, so the front of the team moves as a unit rather than leaving gaps for the opposition to play through.

## Common mistakes

- Using a high-Flair creator with poor Work Rate. He barely contributes to the press and the opposition plays through him freely.
- Combining it with a very aggressive press trigger and an unfit player. The press fades after the first few minutes and the gap between the lines opens.
- Treating it as a defensive role. If you need him to track back and defend in the block, switch to the Tracking Attacking Midfielder.
