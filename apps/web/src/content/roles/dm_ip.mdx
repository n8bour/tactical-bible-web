## What it does by phase

Holds position in front of the defence; circulates the ball carefully.

In the build-up he offers a short, safe passing option just in front of the centre-backs and moves the ball on with the minimum of fuss. In progression and the final third he stays behind the ball rather than joining the attack, so he is the player the opposition breaks against first when you lose it. Think of him as the fixed point the rest of the team's shape is measured from. In the lab, watch how little his position changes between phases compared with the central midfielders around him.

## How attributes change it

Tackling, Positioning and Anticipation matter most because this role spends its whole life reacting to what happens when possession is lost. Anticipation shortens the delay before he reads a turnover, and Positioning keeps him on the right spot in front of the back line instead of a few metres off it. Teamwork decides how tightly he holds the tactical position instead of following the ball, and Concentration keeps his read of opponents accurate late in a game. Passing and Composure are the preferred extras: they decide whether his simple distribution under pressure actually finds its target.

## Pairs well with

The anchor of the rest-defence. Almost every tactic has at least one of these "safe" players.

- A Wide Covering Central Midfielder or Box-To-Box Midfielder next to him: he stays behind while the partner is free to travel.
- Inside Full-Back or Half Back style roles who tuck in, making a compact base so he is not the only cover.
- Creative roles ahead of him such as Advanced Playmaker, so he does not need to create anything.

## Common mistakes

- Asking him to be the team's playmaker. If the build-up needs creativity, use a Deep-Lying Playmaker instead and keep a second safe midfielder beside him.
- Fielding two or three of these in the same midfield, which leaves too many players holding position and no one to progress the ball.
- Picking a player with low Positioning or Anticipation because he is "big and tough". He will be a step late to every turnover.
