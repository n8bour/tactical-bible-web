## What it does by phase

Key moment (final): goes to the byline to cross.

He stays wide, receives the ball on the touchline, and takes on the full-back. In the final third he goes to the byline and crosses. He gives the team its width and its main route to the box, and his job is to get the ball into the area for the forwards.

## How attributes change it

Crossing, Dribbling and Technique decide the quality of his end product: Crossing sets how accurate the delivery is, Dribbling lets him beat the full-back, and Technique controls the weight of the ball. Acceleration, Agility and Pace give him the burst to beat the defender and reach the byline. Off the Ball, First Touch and Flair are the preferred extras that help him create chances and make him less predictable.

## Pairs well with

Pair with targets in the box (CF, TF, Poacher).

- A Target Forward, Centre Forward or Poacher in the box to attack the crosses.
- A Box-To-Box Midfielder arriving as a second wave to meet cut-backs.
- A Full-Back or Wing-Back who stays slightly behind, so that the winger is the only one on the flank.

## Common mistakes

- Putting him in a team with no one in the box. The crosses will have nobody to attack.
- Pairing him with an Advanced Wing-Back on the same side. They compete for the same touchline.
- Playing a winger with poor Crossing or Dribbling. He will lose the ball or deliver poorly.
