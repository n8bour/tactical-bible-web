## What it does by phase

Holds position and width; supports without bombing forward constantly.

He holds width on the touchline and supports the attack with passes and occasional overlaps, but does not bomb forward constantly. When the winger comes inside he provides the width. His main contribution is a safe outlet who keeps the defensive structure intact.

## How attributes change it

Marking, Tackling, Positioning, Concentration, Anticipation and Teamwork make him a full-back who holds his position. Crossing, Passing and Decisions help in the moments he does go forward; Work Rate and Stamina let him get up and back. In the lab, higher Teamwork keeps him closer to his tactical position.

## Pairs well with

Safe. Pair with a winger who comes inside to keep the width.

A winger who comes inside, such as an Inside Winger or Inside Forward, so the full-back holds the width. A Central Midfielder or Wide Central Midfielder who supports the flank covers him. Two simple centre-backs behind keep the rest-defence stable.

## Common mistakes

- Expecting him to deliver crosses all the time; he does not push high.
- Pairing him with a Wide Forward who stays wide; nobody is inside.
- Ignoring Stamina when asking for repeated overlaps.
