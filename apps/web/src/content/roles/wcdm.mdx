## What it does by phase

Key moment (oop): moves across to cover the flank.

When the full-back or wing-back on one side pushes forward, he moves across to cover the flank behind him. He shifts from his central position into the wide channel so that a counter down that side does not meet an empty space. This is a disciplined, repeated shuffle across the pitch, not a one-off run.

## How attributes change it

Work Rate and Stamina are essential because he constantly moves laterally and has to cover a lot of ground. Positioning keeps him on the right side of the gap, and Teamwork keeps the shape intact when he leaves the middle. Anticipation and Tackling let him react to a break and win the duel on the flank. Acceleration helps him get across to the wide area before the winger arrives.

## Pairs well with

The natural partner of a very attacking wing-back.

- An Advanced Wing-Back or Wing-Back: the more the full-back goes forward, the more important this cover becomes.
- A Central Midfielder or Box-To-Box Midfielder on the other side, who holds the middle while the Wide Covering Defensive Midfielder moves across.
- A Holding Full-Back on the opposite flank, who stays at home.

## Common mistakes

- Using him with Full-Backs who never move forward. He has nothing to cover and the role does nothing.
- Selecting a low Work Rate or Stamina player. He will be a step slow once the match tires him.
- Doing the same on both flanks, which empties the centre.
