## What it does by phase

Key moment (final): occupies zone 14 and threatens from distance.

He occupies the space between the lines, especially the area in front of the penalty box known as zone 14, and threatens goal himself. This is the old attacking central midfielder at MC: he links play with short passes but also takes shots from distance. He arrives in the box as a second wave to the forwards, and drops back into the line when the ball is lost.

## How attributes change it

Long Shots, First Touch and Passing define him: First Touch lets him control and turn between the lines, Long Shots makes the shot from distance a real option, and Passing keeps him linking the play. Anticipation and Off the Ball set the timing of his arrival in the box and help him avoid offside. Decisions and Flair decide whether he shoots, passes or tries something unexpected. Dribbling, Finishing and Composure are the preferred extras for when he gets into the box.

## Pairs well with

Great for arriving in the box as a second wave.

- A Target Forward or Centre Forward who holds the ball up and draws the centre-backs, so the second-wave arrival is unmarked.
- A Wing-Back or Winger who crosses, since the attacking midfielder is arriving in the box as the ball is delivered.
- A Defensive Midfielder and a Central Midfielder behind him, who protect the space he leaves.

## Common mistakes

- Using him with two other attackers who all want the same zone. The area between the lines gets crowded.
- Expecting a playmaker. His main aim is to threaten goal, so if you want the team organised from there, use an Advanced Playmaker.
- Picking a player with low Long Shots and Finishing. The role relies on shooting threat.
