## What it does by phase

Key moment (oop): covers the channel, deeper.

In a back 3 the wide centre-back defends reactively and stays slightly deeper, covering the channel between the flank and the central centre-back. He is cautious rather than a presser, so he holds shape when the wing-back advances. The role is a safety net for the side of the pitch with the most space.

## How attributes change it

Positioning, Anticipation and Concentration let him read the channel and arrive before the runner. Pace is critical because he often has to chase a runner who has beaten the wing-back. Marking is preferred for dealing with the forward in his channel, and Tackling and Decisions let him act when needed. Low Positioning shows as the wrong slot in the lab.

## Pairs well with

Protect the back of a very attacking wing-back.

A very attacking wing-back or Advanced Wing-Back, whose back he protects. A Stopping Wide Centre-Back on the other side if you want asymmetry, and a Covering Centre-Back in the middle for balance. A Wide Covering Defensive Midfielder is another source of flank cover.

## Common mistakes

- Using him with a cautious Holding Wing-Back; two defensive players on the same flank waste attacking width.
- Choosing a slow defender and then asking him to cover the channel.
- Forgetting he is a reactive role, so he will not win the ball high up the pitch.
