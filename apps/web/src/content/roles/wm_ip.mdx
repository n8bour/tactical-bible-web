## What it does by phase

Holds position and width, a balancing role.

He holds a wide position and gives the team width, a balancing role in a flat midfield. He does not cut inside or run beyond; he stays on the flank, receives the ball, and either passes inside or crosses. This is the classic 4-4-2 wide midfielder: simple, disciplined and positionally reliable.

## How attributes change it

Passing, Crossing and Teamwork matter most: Crossing sets how accurate his deliveries into the box are, Passing keeps the ball moving when he receives it, and Teamwork holds him to his position on the flank. Work Rate and Stamina cover the running required up and down the touchline. Decisions help him choose between crossing and passing inside. Off the Ball, First Touch, Technique and Tackling are preferred attributes that improve his contribution in both phases.

## Pairs well with

Classic 4-4-2.

- A Full-Back who overlaps rarely, or a Wing-Back who covers the line behind him.
- Two central midfielders and a pair of strikers in a classic 4-4-2, where the flank is the main source of width.
- Target Forward or Centre Forward in the box, who attack his crosses.

## Common mistakes

- Expecting him to be a game-changer. He is a balancing role, so if the team needs flair on the flank use a Winger or Inside Winger.
- Playing him with a Winger or Wide Forward in front of him, which duplicates the width.
- Using him with poor Crossing. His main output is the cross, and a poor delivery is a lost ball.
