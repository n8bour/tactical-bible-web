## What it does by phase

Key moment (final): arrives in the box as a second wave.

He covers the whole length of the middle: helping the build-up, linking in the middle third, and pushing up into the attacking-midfield zone so that he can arrive in the box as a second wave behind the strikers. The run is timed late, often after a cross or a through ball is already on its way. Without the ball he has to come back, which is why he needs the engine to do both jobs every game.

## How attributes change it

Stamina and Work Rate come first. Without them he is exhausted by the hour mark and either stops making the late run or stops covering back. Off the Ball and Anticipation set the quality of his box arrival: a good run start is on time and avoids offside, a poor one is early or late. Passing, Tackling and Teamwork keep him useful in the central phases between the two penalty areas. Dribbling, Finishing, Long Shots and First Touch are the preferred attributes because they make the second-wave arrival actually dangerous.

## Pairs well with

Stamina and Work Rate are non-negotiable.

- A Defensive Midfielder or Half Back beside or behind him, who stays at home so the run does not leave the middle empty.
- Forwards who occupy the centre-backs, such as a Target Forward or Poacher, so the arrival from deep is unmarked.
- Wingers or Wide Forwards who cross early, giving him a reason to arrive late in the box.

## Common mistakes

- Playing him with low Stamina or Work Rate. The role loses its point after an hour, and the shape behind him collapses.
- Using two Box-To-Box Midfielders without a holding player, which strips the rest-defence.
- Judging him only on goals. The role is about covering ground and supporting both boxes, not about a fixed output.
