## What it does by phase

Key moment (prog): steps into midfield.

When the team has the ball in the progression phase he steps from the back line into the defensive-midfield zone, which creates a central overload and an extra passing option. He is not a runner; he joins the midfield only to circulate the ball and shift play. The back line is therefore down a man in possession.

## How attributes change it

Passing, First Touch, Technique, Composure and Vision matter because he is playing as a midfielder under pressure, in tight space. Decisions and Anticipation tell him when it is safe to step forward and when to drop back, which is the thing that separates a good ACB from a liability. Positioning and Tackling keep him a defender when the ball is lost. Teamwork helps him hold his place in the shape and Stamina lets him recover.

## Pairs well with

Make sure of cover: with an ACB, leave 2 centre-backs/IFB behind or a Half Back.

Two other centre-backs or an Inside Full-Back behind him, or a Half Back who covers the gap. A Ball-Playing Goalkeeper helps because he builds from deep. A Defensive Midfielder who drops wide frees him to take the central pivot spot.

## Common mistakes

- Using him in a back 4 with attacking full-backs: nobody is left to defend the counter.
- Choosing a defender with weak Passing and Composure, who loses the ball at the worst spot.
- Combining him with a Half Back in the same area; they will occupy each other's zone.
