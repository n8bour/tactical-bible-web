## What it does by phase

Key moment (final): moves into the half-space to create.

He starts wide but moves into the half-space between the full-back and the centre-back to create. From there he can carry the ball, play the through ball, or combine with the forwards. He is more creative than an Inside Forward, who aims to shoot, and less of a deep organiser than a Playmaking Winger.

## How attributes change it

Dribbling, First Touch and Technique let him receive and turn in the tight spaces of the half-space. Composure helps him choose well under pressure and Teamwork keeps him linked to the team's structure. Acceleration and Agility help him break away from his marker. Passing, Vision, Off the Ball and Decisions are the preferred extras that turn the position into chance creation.

## Pairs well with

The full-back on the same side should give width (WB/AWB).

- A Wing-Back or Advanced Wing-Back on the same side, who provides the width the winger gives up.
- A Centre Forward or Target Forward who holds the centre-backs, so the half-space is open.
- A Full-Back who overlaps rather than tucks in, so the flank is not abandoned.

## Common mistakes

- Pairing him with an Inside Full-Back. With nobody wide, the team loses its width and the flank empties.
- Using him in a team that does not want to play through the centre, where he will be crowded.
- Choosing a player who prefers to stay wide. The movement inside is the role.
