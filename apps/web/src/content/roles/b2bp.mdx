## What it does by phase

Key moment (final): pushes up to create between the lines.

He starts deep but advances into the attacking-midfield zone, where he looks for pockets of space between the opposition's lines. Once there he tries to create with passes instead of arriving in the box himself, which makes him the creative counterpart to the Box-To-Box Midfielder. Behind him he leaves a gap, so the rest-defence has to be covered by another midfielder.

## How attributes change it

Passing, Vision and Decisions drive the creative half of the role: Vision opens longer passes and allows through balls, and Decisions decides whether he picks the best one. First Touch and Technique let him play on a tight turn between the lines. Work Rate and Stamina are listed as key because he must make the journey from deep to advanced and then recover, repeatedly. Off the Ball, Anticipation, Dribbling and Long Shots are the extras that make him a threat when he finds space himself.

## Pairs well with

Good in a double pivot with a safe DM.

- A Defensive Midfielder in a double pivot: he provides the safe base while the playmaker moves up.
- Inside Wingers or Inside Forwards, whose movement into the half-spaces gives him target options between the lines.
- A Target Forward or Centre Forward who holds the line, so the final pass has a destination.

## Common mistakes

- Putting him in a midfield with no one to hold the base. He will leave the team exposed whenever the ball is lost.
- Giving him no forward options. A creator needs runners ahead of him, otherwise the passes get recycled.
- Using a low Stamina player. He will stop making the advanced move before the second half.
