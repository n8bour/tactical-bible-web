## What it does by phase

Key moment (oop): holds the back 5.

He holds position in the back 5 instead of tracking or pressing. He sits close to the centre-backs, which keeps the back line compact and deep. The flank in front of him is left to the wide midfielder.

## How attributes change it

Positioning, Concentration, Marking and Decisions keep him in the right spot and make him reliable in a low block. Tackling, Anticipation and Strength are preferred for the duels that remain. In the lab, a high Positioning value keeps him close to his defensive slot.

## Pairs well with

Low block 5-4-1 / 5-3-2.

A low block with a 5-4-1 or 5-3-2, with a Covering Wide Centre-Back or Covering Centre-Back inside him. A Tracking Wide Midfielder or Wide Covering Central Midfielder in front gives the flank pressure. Combine it with a deep Defensive Line.

## Common mistakes

- Using him in a high line; he cannot press and leaves a gap.
- Leaving the winger free with no midfielder in front.
- Expecting him to win the ball back; he is a holder.
