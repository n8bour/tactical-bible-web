## What it does by phase

Key moment (final): pushes up the flank and gives width.

He pushes up the flank to give width and cross, appearing often in the final third. The team's wide players can come inside because he occupies the touchline. He leaves space behind him when possession is lost.

## How attributes change it

Crossing, Dribbling and Acceleration let him create and beat a defender in an advanced position. Stamina, Work Rate and Teamwork keep him going and keep the shape. Marking and Tackling remain relevant because he must defend the whole flank. Off the Ball helps the timing of his overlap.

## Pairs well with

Needs high Stamina. Pair with an Inside Forward/Inside Winger ahead.

An Inside Forward or Inside Winger ahead of him, who leaves the flank to him. A Wide Central Midfielder or Channel Midfielder covers behind. A Holding Wing-Back on the other side balances the structure.

## Common mistakes

- Putting a low-Stamina player in the role.
- Pairing with a Winger who also stays wide; the flank gets crowded.
- Leaving no cover behind: without a Covering Wide CB or a cover midfielder the flank is wide open.
