## What it does by phase

Midfield organiser, expressive, demands the ball.

He is the midfield organiser. He constantly asks for the ball, moves into pockets to receive, and plays the creative pass instead of the simple one. Because the role is expressive, he will try riskier forward passes and long switches more often than a standard central midfielder. He links play like a Central Midfielder, but with the aim of creating rather than just keeping the ball.

## How attributes change it

Passing, Vision and Decisions are the creative engine. Vision sets how far he can see and whether he recognises a through ball at all; Decisions sets how often he takes the best option from the choices he finds. First Touch and Technique let him receive on the half-turn and weight the ball, and Composure keeps the pass accurate when a midfielder is on his back. Teamwork keeps him in a position where teammates can find him. Flair, Off the Ball and Anticipation are the preferred attributes because they decide how bold his choices are and how well he finds space.

## Pairs well with

A single central creator per tactic is usually enough.

- A Defensive Midfielder or Central Midfielder beside him to do the covering and the simple passing.
- Forwards who run beyond the defence, such as a Channel Forward or Poacher, so there is always a pass worth making.
- Wide players who stretch the opposition, giving him more room to work in the centre.

## Common mistakes

- Fielding two playmakers in the same central pair, with no one to cover or keep it simple.
- Putting him in a low-possession, direct tactic where the ball rarely gets to him.
- Picking a player with strong Flair but poor Decisions or Composure. He will try the hard pass and lose the ball.
