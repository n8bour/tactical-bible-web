## What it does by phase

Plays it safe: clears, goes long, avoids risks in the build-up.

He treats every restart as a way to remove danger: goal kicks go long, and under pressure he clears rather than finds a teammate. He does not step off his line to support the build-up and does not try to thread passes through the first line. The aim is to push the opponent's press back and give your forwards a contested ball to win.

## How attributes change it

Kicking sets the distance and accuracy of the long restarts and clearances, so it is the main attribute for this role. Handling, Reflexes, Aerial Reach and Positioning keep him a safe shot-stopper and a reliable claimer of crosses, which is the other half of the job. Composure and Command of Area let him stay calm on loose balls and take charge of crosses; Strength helps in aerial contests. A keeper with poor Passing is perfectly fine here, which is the main reason to choose the role.

## Pairs well with

A good partner for "Bypass Press" and Goal Kicks "Long" with a Target Forward.

Bypass Press and Goal Kicks "Long" with a Target Forward or Deep-Lying Forward winning the first ball, plus a Defensive Midfielder who picks up the second ball. No-Nonsense Centre-Back in front of him completes a coherent direct style. It also fits a low block, where the aim is to relieve pressure rather than keep the ball.

## Common mistakes

- Combining him with "Play Through Press" or a short-passing identity; the keeper contradicts the plan.
- Using him with forwards who cannot win aerial duels, so every long kick is simply a turnover.
- Choosing him when your keeper actually has good feet; you give away the build-up advantage for no benefit.
