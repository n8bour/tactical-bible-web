## What it does by phase

Standard midfield defending.

Out of possession he is a standard midfielder in the middle line, shifting with the ball and closing space between the lines. He will challenge when the ball comes close, but he neither drops into the defence nor goes hunting for the ball. He is the base option that the more specific variants are measured against.

## How attributes change it

Tackling, Positioning and Decisions are the base of the role: Positioning keeps him in the right slot in the line and Decisions helps him pick when to engage. Teamwork keeps the midfield compact so there are no gaps between players. Work Rate sets how far he follows the ball before he returns to his zone. Anticipation, Concentration, Marking and Stamina are the preferred attributes that sharpen his positioning over a full match.

## Pairs well with

The base option.

- A Defensive Midfielder in a double pivot, who holds the base while he covers more ground.
- A Pressing Central Midfielder or Screening Central Midfielder as the other half, depending on whether the team wants to press or sit.
- A standard back four and a mid block, where the central midfielder is neither the first presser nor the last line.

## Common mistakes

- Leaving every midfielder at the default. The team has no pressing trigger and no screen, so it does neither well.
- Selecting a player with low Work Rate. The midfield line breaks as soon as he stops closing down.
- Ignoring Teamwork. A midfielder who drifts out of the line creates the gap the opposition wants.
