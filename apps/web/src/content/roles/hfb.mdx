## What it does by phase

Key moment (oop): stays deep and narrow protecting the centre-back.

He stays deeper and narrower than a standard full-back, sitting close to the centre-back to protect against direct balls and cut-backs. He joins the attack late, if at all, and leaves the winger more space to receive wide. The flank is defended by position rather than by tackling.

## How attributes change it

Positioning, Concentration, Marking, Decisions and Strength make him a compact defender who is hard to beat in the penalty-area zone. Tackling, Anticipation and Heading are preferred because he faces crosses and cut-backs. In the lab, a low Positioning value shows as a less precise slot, which is the main thing to avoid in a holding role.

## Pairs well with

Good with the opposite side pressing high (asymmetric structure).

A Pressing Full-Back on the other side builds an asymmetric structure where one side presses and the other holds. A Defensive Midfielder who covers wide and a Covering Centre-Back give the flank extra protection. It suits a team that wants a three-man line in the box when the opposition crosses.

## Common mistakes

- Using him against a winger who is dangerous one-on-one; he sits too deep to engage early.
- Putting holding players on both flanks of a team that needs to press.
- Choosing a defender with poor Positioning and Concentration, which defeats the purpose.
