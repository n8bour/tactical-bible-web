## What it does by phase

Drops into the midfield line of 4/5.

Out of possession he drops into the midfield line of a back four or five, shifting with the ball to protect his flank. He tracks the opposing full-back or winger when they run, but he does not defend as deep as a tracking wide midfielder. He is the base option for a wide midfielder who has to defend.

## How attributes change it

Work Rate and Teamwork decide how well he fills the midfield line: Work Rate scales how far he follows the runner, and Teamwork keeps him in his place in the shape. Positioning keeps him on the correct side of the opposing full-back, and Stamina lets him keep doing it. Tackling, Marking and Decisions are preferred because they improve his duels against the full-back.

## Pairs well with

The base option.

- A Full-Back who sits behind him and shares the flank.
- A central midfield pairing that stays compact, so the wide midfielder does not have to cover the middle.
- A Wide Outlet Wide Midfielder on the opposite side, if you want one flank to stay high.

## Common mistakes

- Expecting him to defend as a full-back. He tracks, but he is still a midfielder.
- Playing a low Work Rate player. The midfield line breaks as soon as he stops tracking.
- Ignoring Teamwork. A wide midfielder who drifts inside leaves his flank open.
