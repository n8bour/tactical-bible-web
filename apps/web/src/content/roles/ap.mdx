## What it does by phase

Key moment (prog): looks for space between the lines.

He moves into the pockets of space between the opposition's midfield and defence, particularly in the progression phase. From there he receives on the half-turn and creates, either with a through ball or a pass into a runner. He plays higher than a Midfield Playmaker and is more of a creator between the lines than an organiser.

## How attributes change it

Passing, Vision and Decisions sit at the centre: Vision widens his range and enables through balls, and Decisions sets how quickly he picks one. First Touch and Technique matter because he receives under pressure in tight spaces, and Composure helps him keep his passing accuracy when marked. Off the Ball and Teamwork determine how well he finds the right pockets and fits into the structure. Dribbling, Flair and Agility are the extras that help him escape pressure and make the unexpected pass.

## Pairs well with

Needs runners ahead of him (IF, CF, Channel Fwd).

- An Inside Forward or Centre Forward who runs beyond, so his through balls have a target.
- A Channel Forward, because the pass into the channel is exactly what he is looking for.
- A Defensive Midfielder and a Central Midfielder behind him, who handle the covering and keep it simple.

## Common mistakes

- Using him without runners ahead of him. He will find space between the lines but have no one to pass to.
- Starting him in a deep, defensive side where the ball rarely reaches the advanced midfield.
- Choosing him with weak Composure. In the tightest part of the pitch, every mistake is a counter-attack.
