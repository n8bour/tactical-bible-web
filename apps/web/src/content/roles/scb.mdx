## What it does by phase

Key moment (oop): steps out of the line to squeeze between the lines.

He steps out of the line aggressively to press the player receiving between the lines, typically the attacking midfielder or a false nine. That stops the opponent turning and keeps play from being easy. It also breaks the line's shape, so someone has to cover the gap he leaves.

## How attributes change it

Tackling, Aggression, Bravery, Anticipation and Decisions are the core, because he wins the ball by timing a step into a challenge. Strength helps him win the body contact and Acceleration lets him close the distance quickly. Marking and Heading are preferred: when he does not win the ball he must stay in the contest. In the lab, higher Anticipation shortens his reaction to a receiver.

## Pairs well with

Excellent against an attacking midfielder/false 9. Needs a Covering partner.

Covering Centre-Back behind him is the classic partner. A Screening Defensive Midfielder in front covers other lanes so he can step. He is excellent against an opposing Attacking Midfielder or False Nine, and a Sweeper Keeper adds extra insurance.

## Common mistakes

- Pairing him with another Stopping Centre-Back; the line collapses on any through ball.
- Using him without Anticipation and Decisions: he steps at the wrong time and is bypassed.
- Using him against a team that plays quickly over the top, where his aggression is a weakness.
