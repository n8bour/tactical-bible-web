## What it does by phase

Key moment (prog): moves inside and organises.

He comes inside and takes over the creating from deep, finding passes between the lines and switching play. He is expressive and likes to try the difficult pass, so he is a playmaker from the wing-back position. His flank is left to the winger.

## How attributes change it

Passing, Vision, Technique, First Touch and Composure make him a playmaker, because every pass he plays comes from a crowded inside lane. Decisions chooses between the safe and the ambitious ball, and Teamwork keeps him in his slot in the shape rather than wandering. Flair raises how often he attempts the risky option, and Off the Ball helps him find pockets of space. In the lab, lowering his Vision shortens the passes he considers and narrows his field of view, so he stops finding the ball between the lines.

## Pairs well with

One per side/tactic at most. Take creative responsibility away from the DLP if you use both.

A winger or Wide Forward who holds the touchline, since he has left the flank. A Defensive Midfielder or Half Back behind him secures the central lane he has joined. Use one per team at most, and take creative responsibility away from the Deep-Lying Playmaker if you use both.

## Common mistakes

- Using one on each side plus a Deep-Lying Playmaker, which leaves too many creators and not enough runners.
- Giving him no wide partner, so the whole flank is empty when he comes inside.
- Picking a player with low Composure or Passing; the role asks him to try difficult passes under pressure.
