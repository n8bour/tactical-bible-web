## What it does by phase

Key moment (oop): pushes up to press the winger.

He steps out aggressively to engage the opposing winger high up the pitch, closing the touchline and forcing a hurried pass or a loss. When the line presses he is the first man to the ball on his flank. The space behind him is left to the centre-back and the wide covering midfielder.

## How attributes change it

Work Rate and Anticipation decide whether he starts the press at the right moment and how far he is willing to chase. Aggression and Tackling decide whether he wins the ball or just fouls. Acceleration is what lets him recover when the winger turns away, which is the main risk of the role. In the lab, higher Work Rate widens his press radius.

## Pairs well with

Pairs with Pressing Trap "Outside". Leaves space in behind — needs Acceleration.

Pressing Trap "Outside", which sends the opponent to the touchline where he is waiting. A Covering Centre-Back or Wide Covering Defensive Midfielder deals with the space behind him. A high Line of Engagement keeps the whole unit close to him.

## Common mistakes

- Using him without Acceleration; the first dribble past him leaves an empty flank.
- Pressing high with no cover behind and a slow centre-back.
- Playing a low-Stamina full-back, who stops pressing after an hour.
