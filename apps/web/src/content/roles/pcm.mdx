## What it does by phase

Key moment (oop): pushes up to press.

Out of possession he pushes up to press the ball and can form the first line of the press with the striker. He leaves the midfield line to close down the nearest opponent, which makes him a Gegenpress or high-block specialist. When the press works, the opposition loses the ball in midfield; when it does not, a hole opens behind him.

## How attributes change it

Work Rate comes first because it scales how far he will go to close the ball and how far he tracks. Aggression and Tackling decide how committed and how clean the challenge is, and a low Tackling number with high Aggression produces fouls. Acceleration lets him reach the ball carrier before the pass is made, and Stamina lets him repeat the sprint. Anticipation chooses the right moment to step out.

## Pairs well with

Gegenpress / high block.

- A Defensive Midfielder or Screening Defensive Midfielder behind him to cover the space he leaves.
- A high defensive line and a high line of engagement, so the press is a team action.
- Forwards who press and cut the passing lanes, so he is closing down a player with limited options.

## Common mistakes

- Using him with a deep defensive line. The press and the line are too far apart and the opposition plays through the gap.
- Playing two Pressing Central Midfielders with no holding midfielder. A bypassed press means a one-on-one with the back line.
- Selecting a low Stamina or Work Rate player. The press fades in the second half.
