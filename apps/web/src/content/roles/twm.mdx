## What it does by phase

Key moment (oop): tracks the opposing full-back all the way back.

He tracks the opposing full-back all the way back into his own half, defending the flank intensely. This role is for low blocks, where the wide midfielder essentially becomes a wing-back out of possession and the team forms a back five or six. He gives up his attacking position to defend the touchline.

## How attributes change it

Work Rate and Stamina are the most important attributes because he has to follow the runner all the way back and then do it again. Teamwork keeps him in the structure, and Tackling and Marking decide how well he wins the duel against the full-back. Positioning keeps him on the right side of the attacker. Acceleration, Anticipation and Aggression are the preferred attributes that help him close the gap.

## Pairs well with

Low block.

- A Full-Back or Holding Full-Back behind him, so the flank has two defenders.
- A low or mid defensive line and a deep line of engagement, so the whole team is compact.
- A forward who stays high, such as a Wide Outlet Winger, to give the team an outlet when the ball is won.

## Common mistakes

- Using him in a high-pressing tactic. He will sit so deep that the press loses its shape.
- Using him on both flanks with no outlet, so the team never has a way out after winning the ball.
- Picking a player with low Stamina. A wide midfielder who tracks all game is exhausted by the second half.
