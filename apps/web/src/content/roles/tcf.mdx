## What it does by phase

Key moment (oop): drops and presses the opposing midfielder.

The Tracking Centre Forward does not wait on the last line. He drops onto the opposing holding midfielder, presses him and helps to block the pass into the middle, and when the team is under pressure he tracks back with the midfield. He is the first defender in a press and an additional midfielder in a compact block, but he starts further from goal when the ball is won.

## How attributes change it

Work Rate sets how far he presses and tracks, and Stamina decides whether he can do it for the whole match. Teamwork keeps him in the team's pressing shape instead of chasing alone, and Anticipation lets him react as soon as the ball is played to the opposing midfielder. Aggression and Bravery make his challenges committed, while Tackling reduces fouls when he does get in the duel.

## Pairs well with

He suits a Gegenpress or compact block. Pair him with a Pressing Central Midfielder, Pressing Defensive Midfielder and Attacking Midfielder (OOP) so the whole press is coordinated, and with a Full-Back (OOP) or Pressing Full-Back on the flanks. A Wide Outlet Winger on one side can keep a counter option alive.

## Common mistakes

- Using him in a team with a low line and a low block where nothing is pressed. He drops back but has little to attack.
- Choosing a striker with weak Work Rate and Stamina. He tires early and the front of the press disappears.
- Combining him with other Tracking roles in every position and leaving no outlet for the counter-attack.
