## What it does by phase

Key moment (build): moves inside, alongside the defensive midfielder.

He holds a deeper position and moves inside into the defensive-midfield zone in possession, which creates a 3-2 in the build-up. He supports circulation rather than width. He gives the team a central presence with a defender's discipline.

## How attributes change it

Passing, First Touch, Technique and Composure matter most, because he receives in a crowded central lane and must pass cleanly under pressure. Decisions and Teamwork keep his choices sensible and his position tied to the structure. Positioning and Tackling are preferred for the moment possession is lost, since he is still a defender. Vision widens what he can see, and in the lab a low value cuts the passes he considers.

## Pairs well with

Creates a 3-2 in the build-up. Needs a winger giving width on the same side.

A winger who gives width on his side, such as a Winger or Wide Forward. A Defensive Midfielder who stays or a Half Back supports the 3-2. An attacking full-back on the other side.

## Common mistakes

- Using him with an Inside Winger on the same side; nobody is wide.
- Picking a wing-back with weak passing.
- Expecting him to defend as a full-back when the ball is out wide.
