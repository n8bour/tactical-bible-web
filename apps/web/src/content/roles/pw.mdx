## What it does by phase

Key moment (prog): starts wide and comes inside to organise.

He starts on the flank and comes inside to organise play, acting as a playmaker from a wide start. The key moment is in progression, when he drifts into the centre to receive and play the creative pass. He leaves the touchline open, so the full-back has to supply width. He is very expressive, which means he will try the difficult pass.

## How attributes change it

Passing, Vision and Decisions drive the creative role: Vision sets how far he can see and whether he spots through balls, Decisions chooses among them, and Passing keeps the delivery accurate. First Touch and Technique let him receive in the half-space, and Composure keeps him calm under pressure. Flair gives him the boldness to try the risky pass. Dribbling, Off the Ball, Anticipation and Agility are the preferred extras for when he has to carry the ball.

## Pairs well with

One creator per flank at most.

- A Wing-Back or Advanced Wing-Back who takes over the flank when he moves inside.
- A Centre Forward or Target Forward who holds the ball up, so he has a target for his passes.
- A Defensive Midfielder who covers the space behind him when the ball is lost.

## Common mistakes

- Putting a Playmaking Winger on both flanks, with a Midfield Playmaker too. The team has too many creators and too few runners.
- Using him with an Inside Full-Back, which leaves no width on that side.
- Picking a player with weak Passing or Decisions. The playmaking role needs both.
