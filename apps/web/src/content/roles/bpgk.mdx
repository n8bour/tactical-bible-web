## What it does by phase

Key moment (build): comes off his line to support the build-up.

In the build phase he steps off his line and takes a position slightly higher than a normal keeper, becoming a passing option that effectively adds an extra man in the first line. He accepts riskier passes, will play into midfield or switch the ball to the far full-back and is the first to receive when the centre-backs split. The price is that when possession is lost near his own box he is further from goal than a standard keeper.

## How attributes change it

Passing, First Touch and Kicking are the core: they set how accurate his short and medium passes are and whether he controls a hard pass cleanly. Composure matters more than usual because his options shrink when a forward closes him down, and Decisions and Vision determine whether he finds the right outlet or forces a pass into traffic. In the lab, lower Vision shortens the passes he considers and narrows his field of view, and he stops seeing through balls altogether.

## Pairs well with

Essential with "Play Through Press". Without Passing/Composure ≥ 12 he is a source of dangerous turnovers.

Ball-Playing Centre-Back and Inside Full-Back, who offer short angles and spread wide so he always has an option. A Deep-Lying Playmaker or Half Back gives him a midfielder who is comfortable receiving with a forward closing behind him. Use him with "Play Through Press" and with a Sweeper Keeper-style high Defensive Line, because both ask the keeper to be comfortable far from goal.

## Common mistakes

- Picking him for the name when Passing or Composure is below about 12; he becomes a source of dangerous turnovers.
- Pairing him with two No-Nonsense centre-backs and a long Goal Kicks setting; the role cannot work if nobody offers a short option.
- Using him against an aggressive press without a free midfielder; every pass becomes a forced, pressured one.
