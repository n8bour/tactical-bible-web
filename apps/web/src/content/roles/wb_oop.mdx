## What it does by phase

Drops back to form a back 5 when needed.

In a back 3 or 5 he drops back to form the outer part of the line when the opposition has the ball, then tracks the opposing wide runner. He covers the whole flank and not just the byline. He bridges between a full-back and a wide midfielder.

## How attributes change it

Marking, Tackling, Positioning, Work Rate, Stamina and Acceleration are the key attributes: he covers the whole flank, so he needs to get back and stay with his man. Anticipation and Teamwork help him decide when to drop and when to step. In the lab, lower Work Rate shortens how far he tracks the winger.

## Pairs well with

The base for a back 3.

It is the base choice for a back 3, with a Covering Wide Centre-Back or Stopping Wide Centre-Back inside. A Wide Covering Central Midfielder or Tracking Wide Midfielder in front shares the flank. Pair him with an Advanced Wing-Back or Wing-Back going forward.

## Common mistakes

- Using him with poor Stamina; the role asks for the longest running on the pitch.
- Playing him in a flat back 4 where there is no back 5 to form.
- Expecting him to press high; that is Pressing Wing-Back.
