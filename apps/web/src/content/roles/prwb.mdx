## What it does by phase

Key moment (oop): pushes up to press.

He pushes up to press the opposing full-back or winger, turning the back 5 into a front-foot defence on his flank. He engages high up the pitch, which gives the opposition less time on the ball. He leaves the channel behind him to the wide centre-back.

## How attributes change it

Work Rate, Aggression, Anticipation, Acceleration and Stamina are the main attributes: he must press repeatedly, close fast and recover. Tackling and Teamwork are preferred so that his press is a good tackle and is synchronised with the rest of the line. In the lab, higher Work Rate widens his press radius.

## Pairs well with

Ideal in a pressing 3-4-3.

A pressing 3-4-3 with a Stopping Wide Centre-Back or Covering Wide Centre-Back behind him. A high Line of Engagement and Trigger Press support the same idea. A Holding Wing-Back on the other side keeps the shape.

## Common mistakes

- Using him in a deep low block; there is nothing to press.
- Choosing a player with low Stamina.
- Leaving the wide centre-back no cover.
