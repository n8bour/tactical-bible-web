1–2 main creators (DLP, MP, AP, FR, PW, PWB). Too many creators = everyone wants the ball, nobody attacks the space.

Creators such as the Deep-Lying Playmaker, Midfield Playmaker, Advanced Playmaker, Free Role, Playmaking Winger and Pressing Wing-Back ask for the ball and look for the killer pass. That is excellent in moderation, but a team of playmakers stops moving and gives its forwards nobody to pass to.

Pick one creator for the deeper area and one for the final third, then surround them with runners: Wingers, Inside Forwards, Channel Midfielders or a Channel Forward who attack space. Check the creators' Vision, Passing and Technique, and keep creative team instructions modest if the rest of the side is not suited to taking risks.
