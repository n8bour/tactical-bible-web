Players who stay behind the ball during the attack to stop the counter-attack. Rule of thumb: 3 to 5 (not counting the GK) with a Positive/Attacking mentality — typically 2 centre-backs + 1 defensive midfielder, or a full-back who tucks inside (IFB/IWB).

A useful rule is three to five players, not counting the goalkeeper, with a Positive or Attacking mentality. Typically that means two centre-backs plus a defensive midfielder or a full-back who tucks inside, such as the Inside Full-Back or Inside Wing-Back. In possession, the Half Back, Inside Full-Back and Inside Wing-Back all carry the rest-defence tag and do this job without extra instructions.

If you are conceding on the counter, check how many players are actually behind the ball when you lose it, not just how many are listed in the formation. The cure is usually one more holding role, not a more cautious mentality for the whole team. Also give the rest-defenders Anticipation and Pace, because they will often be defending in space.
