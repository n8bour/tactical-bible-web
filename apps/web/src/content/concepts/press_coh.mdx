The line of engagement (LoE) and the defensive line must move together. High LoE + deep line = a hole between the lines. Low LoE + high line = space in behind with no pressure on the pass. Pressing Trap Outside needs someone to close the channel (Pressing FB/WB, Tracking Winger).

If your line of engagement is high but your defensive line is deep, a large gap opens between the lines and the opposing midfield receives the ball in space. If the line of engagement is low but the defensive line is high, there is space behind the defence and no pressure on the passer. Set the two so the distance between them is sensible, and move them together when you change one.

Pressing Trap Outside needs somebody to close the channel on the touchline, such as a Pressing Full-Back, Pressing Wing-Back or Tracking Winger. Aggressive pressing roles like the Pressing Defensive Midfielder also need fit players with high Work Rate and Stamina, otherwise the team presses in pieces and the opposition plays around it.
