FM27 measures complexity (team, player and opposition instructions). The more complex it is, the more capable the players and the more training time they need. In the lower leagues, simplicity wins games.

Every extra team, player and opposition instruction adds to the complexity of the tactic. A more complex plan can be more precise, but only if the squad has the ability and the time to learn it. A team of strong senior players adapts quickly, while an inexperienced or low-level squad can be overwhelmed by a long list of instructions.

In practice, start with a clear shape, a small number of well-chosen roles and a few team instructions that express one idea, such as pressing high or playing direct. Add player instructions only where a role does not already do what you want. In the lower leagues, a simple tactic that everyone understands will usually beat a clever one that the squad cannot execute.
