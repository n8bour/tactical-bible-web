Replaces Tactical Familiarity. Four pillars: Tactical Understanding, Player Ability, Player Buy-In and Next Match Information. Visible in Training, on the tactics screen, the squad, the database and scouting reports (“Projected Effectiveness”).

Tactical Effectiveness combines Tactical Understanding, Player Ability, Player Buy-In and Next Match Information. It tells you how well the squad can actually execute the tactic you have set, not just whether you changed it recently. You can see it in Training, on the tactics screen, in the squad and database views and as Projected Effectiveness in scouting reports.

Use it as a guide when you are choosing between a tactic that is tactically ideal and one the players already know. Changing too many roles and instructions at once lowers understanding, while buy-in and match information can be improved by communicating with the squad and scouting the opponent. A small adjustment to a familiar system is often worth more than a complete rebuild.
