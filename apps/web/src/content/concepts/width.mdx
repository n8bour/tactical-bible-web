Each flank needs ONE player giving width in the final third (W, WF, WB, AWB, WCM, OCB). If the winger comes inside, the width must come from behind. Two wide players in the same channel get in each other’s way.

The width provider can be a Winger, Wide Forward, Wing-Back, Advanced Wing-Back, Wide Central Midfielder or Overlapping Centre-Back. If the winger comes inside, as an Inside Winger or Inside Forward does, the width must come from the full-back or wing-back behind him. Two wide players in the same channel get in each other's way and make the attack easier to defend.

Check each side separately: whoever is wide on the left and right should be different from the person who is cutting inside. When you set a narrow team width, make sure the full-backs still have the Crossing and Stamina to provide the only width on the flank.
