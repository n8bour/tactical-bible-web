Since FM26, every player has an in-possession role (IP) and an out-of-possession role (OOP), and the team has a formation for each phase. There are no duties or fluidity: behaviour comes from the role + instructions. The game warns you if the distance between a player’s IP and OOP positions is too large — that creates holes in transitions.

There are no duties or fluidity settings: behaviour comes from the role plus your instructions. That means a full-back can play as an Inside Wing-Back in possession and as a Holding Full-Back out of possession, or a winger can attack as an Inside Winger and defend as a Tracking Winger. Plan both halves together, because the player has to move from one position to the other in transitions.

The game warns you when the distance between a player's IP and OOP positions is too large. Treat that warning seriously: a big gap leaves holes in the moments after you win or lose the ball. Choose IP and OOP pairs that keep the player in the same part of the pitch, and use the formation for each phase to show where the team actually wants to stand.
