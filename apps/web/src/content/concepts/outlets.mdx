OOP “Outlet” roles stay forward for the counter-attack and do not defend. 1–2 give you an outlet; 3+ leave the block outnumbered.

Outlet roles such as the Central Outlet Centre Forward, Splitting Outlet Centre Forward, Wide Outlet Winger, Inside Outlet Winger and Central Outlet Attacking Midfielder remain high up the pitch when you lose the ball. That gives you a target for a quick long pass when you win it back, but it also removes them from the defensive effort.

One or two outlets make a team dangerous on the break. Three or more leave the block outnumbered in midfield. If your team presses high, you may not want any outlets, because the press itself creates the transitions. The Tracking roles, such as the Tracking Winger, Tracking Wide Midfielder and Tracking Centre Forward, are the opposite choice, committing the player to defensive work.
