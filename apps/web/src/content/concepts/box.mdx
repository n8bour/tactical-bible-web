At least 2–3 players attacking the box (striker + inside winger/AM/BBM). With a false 9 or DLF, someone has to attack depth (IF, CHM, SS). In FM27 the new box-attacking instructions (near/centre/far post, arrive late) let you coordinate who goes to each zone.

A cross or cut-back is only dangerous if several players are attacking different parts of the box. A typical set is a Centre Forward or Poacher on the front post, an Inside Forward or Inside Winger arriving at the far post, and a Box-To-Box Midfielder or Wide Central Midfielder running late into the area. If the main striker is a False Nine or Deep-Lying Forward, who drops away from the area, a Second Striker or Channel Midfielder has to provide that depth.

The FM27 box-attacking player instructions let you give each runner a zone. Assign near post, centre and far post to different players, and use arrive late for the midfielder so he does not crowd the striker. Overloading one zone with three players leaves the others empty and makes the cross easy to defend.
